# Owner feedback — 2026-07-02 — Movement feel is the game

> Verbatim-adjacent record of design direction from the studio owner (Devin). This is the
> highest-authority design input we have. The game-designer role must reconcile every combat/feel
> spec against this document.

## The thesis

**This game will be fun or not depending on whether the feel of the movement and combat is
enjoyable.** Reference point: **TowerFall Ascension** — movement so polished it makes you feel
capable and cool because of the amazing stuff you can pull off. GottaBounce needs that caliber of
movement mechanics.

## Proposed method: movement courses as the tuning instrument

Design a series of difficult movement challenge courses where the accomplishment itself feels
good — then tune the movement *around what makes those courses feel great*. Course sketches:

1. **Long-haul fuel management:** a horizontally-stretched level of very tiny platforms, A→B by
   jetpack, barely enough fuel to reach each next platform; flying well lets you refuel by hitting
   platforms on the way; the final leg requires a super boost.
2. **Wall-climb:** two tall thin vertical bounce platforms, far too tall to fly up — super-blast
   left-right-left-right all the way to the top.
3. **Precision smash:** a soccer ball suspended mid-air; super-blast-smash it at the right angle
   through a hoop/goal.
4. **Momentum banking:** bounce harder and harder, then with all that momentum fly off toward a
   far-away goal you couldn't normally reach on fuel alone.
5. **Volleyball relay:** a ball plus multiple players spread far apart, knocking it through the
   air to each other, bumping it up and over a net.

Level-design note (owner's own caveat): these introduce one mechanic at a time — e.g. teach normal
boost + super boost flying *before* the course requiring chained normal boosts + a super finish.

## Super-boost redesign hypothesis (evaluate seriously)

Maybe designing those courses reveals the super boost feels better if it is **not charged**:

- Works **any time you have any fuel left** — even 5%, even 0.1s of aiming yields the same big
  boost.
- You still **aim it, frozen briefly in the air** while aiming; max aim time ~2 seconds.
- Skill expression this unlocks:
  - Boost long-distance on 95% of fuel, then super with the last 5% to go even further.
  - Wall-climb course (#2 above) becomes possible.
  - Miss a super at an opponent, hit the bounce surface behind them, **immediately super again**
    back the other way for the second-try hit.
  - Players stop hoarding supers — freely super to dodge an attack, bounce off a wall, counter-
    strike-super back.

## Super boost = THE kill move

Maybe **super-boosting into another player is the only reliable way to knock them flying**. Other
contact just knocks both players back with normal physics — but a super launches the victim. It
becomes the main killing strategy (and the main way to comically blast *yourself* into a hazard).

## Theming: crazy goats

Lean into the super-boost identity: you fly through the air to smash people head-first — so the
characters could be **crazy goats with horns**. A **goat scream** plays when the super fires.
Wacky humor layer. (Art/audio direction to evaluate.)

## Mode seeds (later)

Sports modes — Rocket League-ish soccer, Brawl Stars-style volleyball/dropshot — fall out of the
ball-course prototypes naturally.

## Standing instruction

These are inputs to evaluation, not decrees — but the *thesis* (movement feel is the product) is
the bar. Pass this forward into every spec review.
