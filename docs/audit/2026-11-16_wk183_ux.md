# WK-183 sports fuel arc: ui-ux-designer verdict (2026-11-16)

Commit `adbb6d6` (`src/ui/SportsFuelArc.gd`, `SportsHUD.gd`, Tuning `wk183_*`). I graded it from the 29 captures in
`tools/out/wk/WK-183_*.png`, with crops enlarged to 2-3x and nearest-neighbour scaling. I compared it with the WK-99
PlayerCard and bubble crops (`WK-99_crop_cards_9_11_50_5.png`, `WK-99_bubble_crop_11_vs_9.png`). The game was not run.

## Verdict: FIX

The direction is right. A carried gauge under each goat answers "which tank is mine?" with no lookup. In every 4-goat
frame you find your goat first, and your tank comes with it. The pieces below are good and stay as they are:
- WK-99 notch semantics
- the refund tick (white slice, width pop, whole-arc glint; it reads even over the red net in the real wall-refund sheet)
- the full-and-steady fade (hold 0.9 s, fade 0.35 s)
- the hide rules (dead, super-aim, goal banner)
- the layering (the ball and goal fronts draw over the arc)

The failure is in the one state the widget exists for. **Near-empty and empty are the quietest reads on screen when
they should be the loudest.** In the live frames, a dry goat's arc is a dark maroon or navy smudge on a night court:
- `WK-183_ds_live_0.png`, BILLY at 0 % fill, only the unlit dot
- `WK-183_ds_live_2.png`, ZIGGY
- `WK-183_ds_p1_superaim_hidden.png`, BUTCH

The low pulse is a full sine down to chocolate (`OUTLINE.lerp(CREAM, 0.75 * pulse)`, where pulse goes 0..1). So for
about half of every cycle the rim is the same chocolate as a healthy tank's, and a glance at that moment says nothing.
The refund ding the backlog asked for is also missing.

## Fix list (in priority order)

1. **The low pulse never goes back to chocolate.** Below `wk183_low_ratio` (0.25), and including 0 fuel, the outline
   mix runs **0.45 -> 0.95** toward CREAM: `rim_mix = 0.45 + 0.50 * pulse`. Today it runs 0 -> 0.75. Keep 2.5 Hz, and
   keep the fill brightening as it is.
   - Net look: a low tank always has a taupe-to-cream rim around a dark track, so it reads as an empty vessel with a lit
     edge.
   - It never reads as full, because the track stays dark. Only the rim lights.
   - Suggested knobs: `wk183_low_rim_floor = 0.45`, and `wk183_low_rim_flash` -> 0.95 as the peak.

2. **Round caps, to match the PlayerCard capsule.** Today `draw_arc` gives butt ends, so a 5 % fill is a square chip
   that reads as a render glitch.
   - Round both ends of the outline band, both ends of the track, and both ends of the fill. Draw a circle of
     radius = half width at each end, in the same colour.
   - The PlayerCard bar and the WK-36 bubble ring are both soft capsules, and this is the Glossy Toybox language.
   - Side effect (wanted): any fuel above 0 shows at least one full round cap, about an 11 px dot. The 5 % and 9 % stubs
     then read as "a drop left", and stop looking like a stray pixel.

3. **Fill width 9 -> 11 px** (`wk183_width_px`). Keep the outline at 2.5 px, so the band becomes 16 px. Keep the
   centre-line radius, so the band grows about 1 px into the body gap and 1 px outward.
   - Reason: about 9 mm at 3 m on a 55" screen is the floor for a shape-read gauge. +2 px buys the low stub and the
     dark track real presence.
   - It stays well under the 40 px name tag, so it does not crowd the ball read.

4. **Separate the notch from the low rim.** With fix 1, the low rim goes cream, and a lit cream notch at the left end
   could merge into it at the pulse peak. Changes:
   - `wk183_notch_overhang_px` 4 -> **5**.
   - Lit notch width 5 deg -> **6 deg** (about 5.5 px).
   - Keep the lit notch's 1.5 px chocolate keyline at every pulse phase. It already has one. Do not lerp it with the rim.
   - Unlit tick: keep it inside the band, but draw it **chocolate at 70 % alpha** instead of `CREAM * wk99_notch_unlit`.
     It is then "absent", a groove in the track, rather than a small grey sibling of the lit bar.
   - Result: LIT is a cream bar that pokes out past both edges, and UNLIT is a dark groove.
   - Lit/unlit already reads at 2x crop (BUTCH 11 % vs BILLY 9 %). This only stops it degrading once fix 1 lands.

5. **Refuel ding (missing; WK-183 asked for it).** No refund or refuel sound exists in `AudioManager`, so the refund
   tick is visual only. "Walls refuel once" (WK-175 WR) and ball refuel are taught by this moment, and it is silent.
   Hand it to the sound-designer:
   - A short bright "glug-ding" (under 120 ms) through `AudioManager`, on the same trigger as the tick (gain >= 1 in a
     frame).
   - Pitch rises with the amount gained: +0 semitones at 10 fuel, up to +5 at 40 or more.
   - Throttle to 1 per 0.15 s per goat.
   - Sit about 10 dB under the ball-hit SFX. A 60 -> 100 burst can use a fuller variant.

6. **Tag vs. arc collisions.** Two cases:
   - (a) `WK-183_ds_live_0.png`: ZIGGY's pip flipped below its goat. Its pointer cuts into the arc at 6 o'clock. In
     sports, the below-anchor must clear the arc's outer edge by 6 px: arc centre radius + band/2 + 6 px in screen
     space. Apply this whenever arcs are enabled, not only while an arc is visible, so the tag does not jump when the
     arc fades.
   - (b) `WK-183_ds_live_1.png`: ZIGGY's tag sits on BUTCH's arc in a vertical stack, so one goat's name sits on another
     goat's tank. In `PlayerPips` living-rect avoidance, extend each goat's rect downward by its visible arc band (about
     +28 px at 0.675 zoom), so the overlap test prefers the clear side.
   - This is the lowest priority on the list. If it does not land with 1-5, file it as its own P3.

## Rulings on the builder's flagged compromises
- **1 (small at 3 m):** fixes 1-3.
- **2 (lit/unlit subtle):** it reads in the crops as is. Fix 4 protects it once the rim lights.
- **3 (arc dips over floor lip or tiles):** accepted. The goat body does the same thing.
- **4 (flipped tag over the arc):** confirmed in the captures. See fix 6.
- **5 (dark empty track on night courts):** keep the team-darkened track (0.70). Flat chocolate was right for the light
  PlayerCard panel but wrong on dark court art, so this deviation is justified. Fix 1 makes the rim do the work where it
  matters.
- **6 (Unlimited Boost fades the arc):** accepted. The cyan flame is the tell.
- **Team colour instead of goat colour:** accepted. Sports tags are team-coloured too, so it is consistent within the
  mode.
