| **Whole ON arm** (`wk108b_arm(true)`) | **KEEP OFF** | No own-goal gain. Stuck +21 % / +49 %, goals/min −24 % all-bot, rallies over 45 s went from 2 to 10 |

**WK-108b Accept is NOT met by any arm:**
- CPU own-goal share ≤ 15 % in all-bot play: best is 17.9 % (K3).

## Setup
- **Build:** `weekend/polish` 60ce151, exported with `git archive` into one scratch copy.
  - The WK-108b switches came in with cb3792a. The 3 commits after it touch Brawl only, so SportsMode, InputBot and the WK-108b part of Tuning match cb3792a.
  - Kickoff gates were on (rule 1) at 3.5 s.
- **Seats:**
  - **b (all-bot):** 4 × InputBot L3. Every seat is a CPU.
  - **h (human-style):** 3 HumanSim3 (skill 0.5, WK-131 CD3 replica) plus 1 CPU at L2, which is player 3, BLUE. This is the seat setup QA-131-2 was measured with.
  - Own-goal targets count only own goals scored by a CPU.
- **Courts:** CENTER PITCH, THE BOWL and THE BOX, each a full match to 6. Plus Dropshot SUNSET BEACH, 5 points.
- **Arms:** all set through `tune_` args. The `GB_WK108B` environment variable was unset.
  - OFF = K1-K4 off, K1b 0, K5 on.
  - ON = K1-K4 on, K1b 2.5, K5 on.
  - K3 = K3 only.
  - K1 = K1 plus K1b 2.5.
  - K5OFF = everything off including K5. This is one Dropshot reference run.
- **Seeds:**
  - OFF and ON: 1-8 on every court and seat setup (step 1: 67-81 goals per court/seat cell, 208-228 per arm × setup).
  - K3 and K1: 1-6 (50-60 goals per cell, 158-169 per arm × setup).
  - Dropshot: seeds 1-2.
  - Every K3/K1 comparison below uses the **seed-matched 1-6** OFF/ON numbers.
- **Machine budget:**
  - ONE headless batch: 2 lanes, 2 Godot instances at most, `--fixed-fps 120`, `--gb-port=0`, a custom user dir, MCP autoload removed in the scratch copy.
  - 177 jobs, 0 SCRIPT ERRORs, 0 stalls, every match ran to MATCH_END.
  - The two Movie Maker recordings ran one at a time after the batch.
- **Metrics:** taken from the builder's probe (`wk108b_probe.gd`). The QA copy has one patch: a goal-mouth stack still open when the stage ends now closes at the end of that stage. The original measured it with the reset round clock.
  - **keeper%:** WK-108 definition. The ball is in the team's own third, and a teammate who is not the nearest goat to the ball is within 300 px of the team's goal.
  - **home300:** anyone on the team within 300 px of its goal.
  - **stack:** at least 3 goats within 450 px of one goal centre while the ball is more than 700 px away.
  - **supporter at T:** from WK-131. The CPU's distance to its own mouth at the gate open. "rank≥1" means the CPU is the supporter, not the chaser.
  - **stuck:** `round_stats.motion_health`, normalised **per bot-minute**.
  - All metrics are game time.
- **Determinism:** the same seed gives the same RNG streams, but runs are not bit-reproducible. Arms are compared as distributions.

## Bugs / findings
1. **MED: QA-108b-1, K1's `keeper_route` drives the keeper through the ball toward its own goal.**
   - Measured: 6 of 9 human-style CPU own goals in the ON arm, and 4-6 of the all-bot own goals in K1/ON.
   - Where: `SportsMode._wk108b_keeper_target` → `_avoid_ball_waypoint` while the keeper is on the wrong side of the ball.
   - Repro: `cpu_own_goal_touches_seed_matched.ON_h` in the summary JSON, field `trole`.
2. **MED: QA-108b-2, K1 raises stuck without delivering keeper presence.**
   - Stuck: +10 % all-bot, +35 % human-style CPU.
   - Keeper%: 14.5 % against a 50 % target; home300 only 35.9 %.
   - The WK-108 keeper% definition can't see a challenging keeper (lessons file). Even home300 doesn't reach 50 % outside THE BOX.
3. **LOW: QA-108b-3, K3 yanks in mouth scrums.** See the recording section: 20 turn-direction reversals in 20 s of 4 bots, against 0 with K3 off.
4. **LOW: QA-108b-4, K5 Dropshot crossings are not literally 0.** 2 objective-path crossings over the net top, both at fuel 0, plus forced ones (stun, super, recovery). See the Dropshot section.
5. **INFO: THE BOX stack metric is partly a kickoff artifact.** The 2v2 slots (x 449 / 1551) are 209 px from the goal centres, inside the 450 px stack radius. 3 of the 7 ON / K3 stacks over 1 s started within 5 s of GO. Judge stacking from recordings.
6. **INFO: QA-131-2 is still open.** The best supporter-at-T number is 38-40 %, from the full ON arm, which is not shippable. K1b on its own path, with K1 off, has not been measured, because K1b is only read by the WK-131 gate-return window.
   - Next step for lane S: a K1b-only arm, with gate_return_lead swept from 2.5 to 3.0.
