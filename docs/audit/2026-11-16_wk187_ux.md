# WK-187 UX review: end-screen mash guard (CD6-05) + WINNER! plate (CD6-10)

Reviewer: ui-ux-designer · 2026-11-16 · commit 2ad8b75 · evidence: `tools/out/wk/WK-187_*` (software GPU, so the
burst's wall-clock stamps run long. Timing below comes from the code arithmetic, not the stamps).

## Verdict: **FIX** (4 small items; the core design ships)

The direction is right and matches the genre. The row stays hidden until the screen has settled, then pops in, and that
pop is the "you can go now" signal. Smash and Mario Kart use the same grammar: the prompt appears when the game is ready
for you. The quiet gap also works. On the brawl_armed_after_mash capture, a pad+keyboard mash ran through the whole
ceremony and the screen was still sitting armed on REMATCH afterwards. Four gaps remain before this ships: one
destructive-input hole, two feedback gaps, and part of CD6-10 that is still open.

## Answers to the three questions

**1. Is about 4 s from the KO too long before a deliberate player can rematch? No. Ship the timing.**
- Brawl, 4 rows: settle = 0.83 + 3 x 0.12 + 0.01 + 0.5 = **1.70 s**. Add the 1.5 s read time and the row arms at
  **3.2 s** of screen time. The screen appears 0.9 s after the KO, so the row arms **4.1 s after the KO**.
- Sports, 4 rows: settle = max(1.61, 1.42) = **1.61 s**, so the row arms at **3.1 s**.
- Genre comparison: Smash Ultimate takes about 5-7 s from GAME! to an actionable results screen. Mario Kart 8 takes
  about 4-6 s to NEXT. Duck Game's scoreboard is longer than either. TowerFall takes about 2-3 s, but it ends rounds,
  not matches. 4.1 s for a **match** end is on the quick side of normal.
- A fast-forward currently gets you there about 1 s sooner (FF at about 0.3 s, then 1.5 s of read). That is fine, but
  see Fix 3: a second deliberate press is currently eaten.

**2. Is the quiet-gap rule invisible-and-fine or confusing? Invisible-and-fine. Do not add a hint.**
- Human mash cadence is 6-10 Hz, which means 50-120 ms up between presses. 0.3 s rejects all of it.
- A deliberate "eager" press at 2 Hz or slower still passes.
- What actually happens at a live row: a masher sees nothing happen, pauses ("huh?"), presses once, and it works. The
  confusion lasts under half a second and fixes itself.
- A "stop mashing" / "release A" line would be programmer-speak on a celebration screen, so don't add one.
- The real missing piece is that the row going live is silent (Fix 2). A couch that is looking at each other, not the
  TV, needs an ear cue that it's live.

**3. Does the plate fit the cream kit? Yes. Ship it.**
- It uses the same ToyPanel as the FINAL SCORE tray: the same cream, the same chocolate rim, and the same corner
  language. On brawl_09 it reads as one product with the tray.
- The gold title keeps its 20 px chocolate outline, so gold-on-cream still reads from across a room.
- At rest (brawl_08/09) the padding is about 45 px at the sides and about 20 px top and bottom, which is right.
- **Verify:** on `WK-187_brawl_armed_after_mash.png` the plate is narrower than on brawl_09 (about 22 px at the sides
  and about 10 px at the top). If that is an older iteration, ignore it. If not, the plate must not shrink with the
  label's pop or bob. Keep it at the scene's offsets (168..832 x 8..168 in label space).
- Sports (BLUE WINS!) needs no plate. The darker court scrim already carries it, and a cream plate would fight the
  team colour.

## Fixes (numbered, in priority order)

1. **[P1] Apply the quiet gap to `back` as well as `accept`, on Victory and SportsVictory only.**
   - Pad B (button 1) is `use_powerup` in gameplay, and by CC-10.1 "B, B" takes **QUIT TO MENU**. A couch mashing
     powerup through the final KO can therefore leave the lobby about 125 ms after the row arms. That is worse than
     the accidental REMATCH CD6-05 was about.
   - Value: the same `Tuning.end_screen_quiet_gap` (0.3 s). Gate the rule by verb, e.g. a `quiet_gap_verbs` list set
     to `["accept","back"]` by the two victory screens. Leave directions ungated.
   - Test: toggle pad B every 50 ms from score 9 until 5 s after MATCH_END. Expected: still on the end screen, focus
     on REMATCH, `mash_ignored > 0`.

2. **[P2] Add a sound to the row going live.**
   - In `_show_row()` (both screens), play `AudioManager.play_sfx("ui_click")` now.
   - Hand the sound-designer a request for a dedicated soft "ready" pop (`ui_ready`, about 80 ms, pitched up from
     ui_click, about -6 dB under it).
   - Today the "ready" moment is visual only, which fails the checklist's feedback rule. This cue is also what keeps
     the quiet gap invisible: people hear "live" and press once.

3. **[P2] Honor a deliberate press while the screen is settled and the row is still hidden, instead of eating it.**
   - The problem: press A to fast-forward, press A again 0.5 s later. Today the second press does nothing and gives
     no feedback. That breaks the "never silently eaten" rule.
   - The fix: when a press passes the quiet gap, the screen has settled (`_ff_done`, or `_t >= settle_time`), and
     `not _row_shown`:
     - play `ui_click`;
     - `rearm_from_now(max(Tuning.end_screen_live_pop, settle_moment + end_screen_read_floor - _t))`;
     - set `_arm_t` to match.
     - Add `var end_screen_read_floor: float = 1.0` to the WK-187 block in Tuning.gd.
   - The row then pops in right away (or as soon as the 1.0 s floor allows). The press itself never activates
     anything: REMATCH still takes its own press on a visible row (CC-10.1).
   - Results:

     | Path | REMATCH available |
     |---|---|
     | Natural (no input) | 4.1 s after the KO (unchanged) |
     | Deliberate A, A | about 2.2-2.6 s after the KO |
     | Mashing | never (unchanged) |

4. **[P3] Finish CD6-10: the frozen in-world goats still show through the ceremony.**
   - brawl_09: P2's world sprite is parked on the top edge of the WINNER! plate, so it looks perched on it.
   - brawl_08/09: the winner's own world sprite pokes out behind the right-hand rays, a duplicate of the bust.
   - sports_t11479: P2's ram sits under the stats panel, directly above QUIT TO MENU.
   - Fix: next to `_set_match_overlays_hidden(true)`, fade every player body, its trail and its pip to alpha 0 over
     0.25 s, in step with the scrim. This is presentation only (INV-12). Restore it on dismiss or rematch.
   - Leave the Brawl scrim at 0.55. The plate solved the title, and the bright toybox world behind is on-brand. Only
     the goats are the tell.
