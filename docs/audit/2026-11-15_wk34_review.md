# UX review - WK-34 Toybox panel kit A (Pause, Options shell + in-match, ScoreScreen) - 2026-11-15 - 8051d4f

UI/UX Designer · lane U · skill `ux-review`. The game was driven live on port 9104 at 1920x1080, plus a 1280x720 resize for the
ScoreScreen. Input was raw pad events on device 0 (d-pad and left stick) and the keyboard as a second joined player (P2).
Covered: the shell Options; a 2 human + 2 CPU BRAWL with pause by each owner device; in-match Options from both pauses; a real
ScoreScreen after a stage; a rematch; and a synthetic 12-KO ScoreScreen at both resolutions.
Evidence: `tools/out/uxr34/*.png` (mine) and `tools/out/wk/WK-34_*.png` (builder). Reference look:
`docs/audit/2026-11-14_lobby_review.md` and the live Herd Lobby (`uxr34/11_lobby.png`).

## Verdict: **FIX**. Ship after F1-F3. Direction approved, no re-architecture.

This is the right kit. All three screens now read as the lobby's product: cream plate, chocolate rim, a coloured ribbon header,
chocolate type, gold focus well, and not one navy panel left. The pause is a good couch pause:
- The header says who paused ("PAUSED · P2" in BUTCH blue with the goat medallion).
- The other player's pad and keys are dead while one player owns the pause.
- RESTART, CHANGE SETUP and QUIT are 0.9 s holds with a ring in the owner's colour, and releasing early drains the ring.
- Backing out of Options puts focus back on OPTIONS.
- The corner HUD cards hide for the whole pause.

Three things stop it shipping. The ScoreScreen prints identity as thin coloured strokes on cream, which the screen across the room cannot
read (F1). The shared ±1° focus wobble makes the long Options rows look crooked in every still (F2). One gameplay overlay still
sits on the "one clean plate" pause (F3).

## MUST-FIX (before WK-34 is called shipped)

**F1. ScoreScreen identity text is unreadable colour-on-cream. Use the kit's own grammar: colour is a FILL, never a thin stroke.** P1 · S · ui-programmer (lane U)
- Evidence: `uxr34/43_score_a.png`, zooms `43z_names.png` and `43z_delta.png`, `50_synth_12ko_1080.png`, `51_synth_12ko_720.png`.
- Contrast measured against the row well `#EBE3D8`:

  | Text | Contrast |
  |---|---|
  | PUFF yellow | 1.21:1 |
  | the "+N" gold delta | 1.14:1 |
  | OOPS! blue | 1.51:1 |
  | ZIGGY green | 1.68:1 |
  | P2 blue | 1.98:1 |
  | P1 pink | 2.22:1 |

  On the white kill chips it is no better: yellow 1.54:1 and green 2.14:1.
- `ToyName`'s `outline_size 6` gives about a 3 px stroke per side at 1080. So PUFF and ZIGGY read as hollow brown outlines with noise inside.
  At 720 (`51`) the "+3 / +2" deltas and OOPS! almost vanish. The delta is the one fact this screen exists to show ("who scored
  this stage"), and it is currently the lowest-contrast mark on the screen.
- It is also the odd one out in the kit. The Pause score chips, the lobby name ribbon, the HUD cards and the stage-intro CPU pips all
  use colour as a block with white or dark type. Rules broken: CC-8.2 (contrast ≥ 4.5:1 or a backplate) and CC-8.3 (identity legible at a glance).
- Do:
  1. **Row name → identity pill.** `H/NameBox/PName` sits inside a PanelContainer with the Pause `ToyChip` style: goat-colour fill,
     3 px chocolate rim, radius 22, content margins 14/4. The label is 32 px white with an 8 px chocolate outline (`ToyChipLabel` at 32).
     The pill is left-aligned and sized to its content. `PSub` (goat name / CPU) stays chocolate at 0.72 below it.
  2. **Delta → pill.** "+N": gold `#FFD23F` fill with chocolate 34 px text, no outline (10:1). "−N": DANGER `#FF4B3A` fill with white text
     and an 8 px chocolate outline. OOPS!: `#9EBDEB` fill with chocolate text (7.5:1). Height 46, 3 px chocolate rim, radius 23. Keep the
     existing 1.6→1.0 TRANS_BACK pop.
  3. **Kill chips → chocolate chips.** `toy_kill_chip` becomes bg `#3B241B` with a 3 px cream `#FFF7EB` rim. KName/VName use the goat
     colour with `outline_size 0`. Contrast on chocolate: yellow 9.4, green 6.8, blue 5.7, orange 5.6, pink 5.1, and purple 3.9 (passes as
     30 px bold large text). The arrow is cream. The SELF-KO tag uses DANGER `#FF4B3A` (4.35:1, large). The OOPS! tag uses `#9EBDEB`. "+N MORE" is cream text.
  4. Re-capture `43`/`50`/`51`-equivalents at both resolutions, and read PUFF and the "+1" from a 25 %-scaled copy of the 1080 frame (the couch test).
  5. WK-35 should reuse items 1 and 2 for the Victory standings rows, so the fix lands once (see L5).

**F2. The focus wobble rotates the long Options rows. In stills they read as crooked, not springy.** P1 · XS · ui-programmer (lane U)
- Evidence: I measured the gold rim's top edge across the focused row: +0.72° (`uxr34/01`), −0.85° (`uxr34/04`), +0.39°
  (`wk/WK-34_07`). On a 1028 px row that is 7-18 px of drop from end to end. The "MUSIC VOLUME" baseline sits 12 px lower than
  its "90%", right next to three dead-level rows and a dead-level ribbon (`04`). Players see that as a layout bug.
- The 1.04 scale also pushes the focused row 27 px past the column on each side, to within ~14 px of the plate rim (`01`). That crowds
  the frame.
- The same ±1° on the 568 px Pause buttons (≈9 px of skew, `20`, `63`) reads as a deliberate toy wobble, so keep it there.
- Rule: rotation must scale with width. Long horizontal elements breathe (scale only) and do not tilt.
- Do:
  - Read a per-control meta in `MenuFX._start_wobble`: `focus_rot_deg` (default 1.0).
  - `OptionsScreen._add_slider` / `_add_toggle` set `focus_rot_deg = 0.0` and register at 1.02 instead of 1.04. The gold well swap
    (6 px rim plus the fill) is the CC-5.1 "full style swap", and the knob's 1.15 pop stays.
  - Shell-wide guard for future wide rows: any control wider than 700 px gets 0°.
  - Re-capture three stills of a focused Options row at different wobble phases. All three must show the row's top edge level to within 2 px.

**F3. The ghost controls legend stays on screen over the paused arena and the in-match Options.** P2 · XS · lane U, coordinating with lane G
- Evidence: builder captures `wk/WK-34_02_pause_pad0.png` and `wk/WK-34_03_options_pad_from_pause.png`. With P1 a ghost, the
  "GHOST! (+) FLOAT (X) PUFF (Y) BAA" chip sits at top-left outside the plate.
- `PartyMatch.set_match_overlays_hidden()` hides only `[_hud, _legend]`. The GhostDirector's `GhostLegend` (and any GhostRing on the
  cards) is not in that list.
- WK-34's own goal ("the paused arena reads as one clean plate") and CC-7.4's "everything stops" both say it goes.
- Do:
  - Add the ghost legend layer to the same hide/restore. Preferably add `GhostDirector.set_overlays_hidden(bool)` and call it from
    `set_match_overlays_hidden`.
  - Re-capture a pause and an in-match Options with a ghosted owner.
  - Lane G is editing `GhostDirector.gd` right now (uncommitted). Hand the one-line hook to lane G, or stage only the PartyMatch hunk.

## Passed (exercised, not assumed)
- **CC-1.5 / 7.2 ownership:** pad-0 pause gives "PAUSED · P1" in pink. Keyboard pause gives "PAUSED · P2" in blue with the BUTCH medallion.
  The non-owner's arrows and d-pad were ignored in the Pause and in the hosted Options (Settings unchanged).
- **CC-1.6 glyphs:** pad owner sees A / B / Start. Keyboard owner sees ENTER / ESC / P. Shell Options swaps ESC↔B chip on the last device.
- **CC-5.1 / 5.2 / 5.3 / 5.6:** exactly one focused element in every capture. The 4-row list wraps both ways. D-pad and left stick both
  navigate and adjust (music 100→90 by stick + d-pad). Backing out of Options restores focus to OPTIONS, in the Pause and in the shell.
- **CC-5.5:** BACK is a non-focusable ribbon chip. B and Esc go back one level.
- **CC-6.1:** a press-and-hold probe on RESTART (pad and keyboard) shows progress at about 1.1/s. Early release drains to 0. A full hold fires
  `restart`. **QA harness note:** a "tap" sent as two separate `gbplay` processes can arrive more than 0.9 s apart on a loaded machine and
  will legitimately confirm RESTART. Probe holds from a single client.
- **CC-7.3:** B, Start, Esc and Options→P resume. Start followed by A within 0.1 s, during the plate pop-in, is honored as RESUME, not eaten.
- **Kill list, tall case:** 12 KOs show 7 chips plus "+5 MORE" in three centred rows. The plate spans y 136-944 at 1080 and stays inside the
  5 % safe area at 720 too. It never touches the HUD cards.
- **R-22:** no BACK or v0.1 over the HUD cards. Nothing is drawn outside the plates (apart from F3).
