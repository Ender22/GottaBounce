# WK-161 Singularity A/B: BR-135 pin breaker in the well (2026-11-16, QA)

**Source:** `docs/audit/2026-11-15_wk135_p2_ab.md` (Singularity pins 0.142 A / 0.108 B, bar ≤ 0.1; stuck 1.46x with BR-135)
· **Data:** `tools/out/wk/WK-161_ab_summary.json` · **Raw per-run JSON, harness, full analysis:** `tools/out/wk/wk161_ab/`

## Verdict: ship neither C nor D. Next, build the obstacle-aware chase target in wells (spec below)

- **Pin bar (≤ 0.1 per bot-stage): every arm fails, B included.** A 0.119, B 0.113, C 0.144, D 0.156. All CIs overlap and no pair
  differs (p ≥ 0.21).
- **The 1.46x stuck regression did not reproduce.** When only the bot switch flips, A vs B is 153 vs 155 episodes (0.99x, p = 0.95).
  - The phase-2 arm B also turned off C2 (`bumper_floor_pays_as_tier`) and C4 (`refund_rearm_timeout`), so the earlier gap most
    likely came from those fuel-economy flips, from noise (phase-2 B's 0.69 was the outlier; A is ~1.0 in both batches), or from
    commits made since (WK-167 and others).
  - So C has nothing to revert. Its stuck ratio is 1.02x B and it adds pins (23 vs 19 for A, n.s.).
- **D (`bot_pin_contacts` 3) is the worst arm.** Pins are 0.156 per bot-stage, and pin breaks double (0.325 per bot-stage).
  - Its extra pins are slow pylon pins: 15 of D's 21 obstacle pins are below 400 px/s, against 3 to 5 in the other arms.
  - With the skip ON, a trip only drops the boost for one reaction tick. Gravity then sets the bot back onto the pylon's outer face,
    and it starts dribbling there.
- **Root cause, from the dump: 84 % of pins (66 of 79, all arms pooled) are a bot thrusting at a target through an obstacle.**
  - 37 are on a **pylon's outer face**. In the radial frame that face is a floor: the bot rests on it while aiming inward at a
    target behind it.
  - 22 are on the core and 7 on a corner block's outer face.
  - The breaker can't fix this. It excludes a face, but the target is still behind it. Routing to the target is the fix.
- **Keep:** BR-135 ON, `wk161_radial_pin_skip` OFF (default), and the dump. The dump should also record the target position;
  see the spec.

## Dump analysis: what the pins and stucks are

**Data:**
- 689 records: 610 stuck, 79 pin. No bot-stage hit the 24-record cap.
- Each record is classified by its `path` (hunt / plan / recover / refuel / aim), hitstun / black-hole flags, the contact object,
  and whether the emitted aim ray hits an obstacle within 400 px.
  - The obstacle test inflates the core circle and the level-JSON rects by the 44 px goat radius.
  - In `hunt` the aim is `_fly_aim(target − pos)`, so an aim through an obstacle means the target is behind it.
- Full tables: `WK-161_ab_summary.json` → `results.<arm>.master_classes` / `dump_classes`.

### Pins (79 pooled; per arm A 16 / B 18 / C 20 / D 25)

| Class | Pooled | Share |
|---|---|---|
| **Aim through obstacle: pylon** (37 of 38 pylon pins on the OUTER face, at x = 292 / 1628) | 37 | 47 % |
| **Aim through obstacle: core** | 22 | 28 % |
| **Aim through obstacle: corner block** (outer face) | 7 | 9 % |
| stun / super-aim (not steering) | 8 | 10 % |
| refuel hop | 3 | 4 % |
| free air | 2 | 3 % |

- **Obstacle share per arm:** A 69 %, B 89 %, C 90 %, D 84 %.
- **A typical pylon pin:**
  - The bot sits at x = 292 (touching the left pylon's outer face) with vx ≈ −310 px/s: the minimum bumper rebound, repeated.
  - Fuel is 90-100 and it is boosting with the aim inward (+x), sliding along y 290-770.
  - Gravity pulls it into the pylon, the bumper kicks it back out, and its thrust pushes it in again: six contacts in a second.
- In A, 6 of 16 pin records had the face already excluded (`brk.ex`), and 3 had `_pin_avoid` steering. The breaker was active, and
  the pin happened anyway.

### Stuck (610 pooled)

| Class | Pooled | Share | Routing addresses it? |
|---|---|---|---|
| Aim through obstacle within 400 px (pylon 67, shell 66, core 51, block 34) | 218 | 36 % | **yes** |
| Free air, but an obstacle on the aim line at 400-1100 px | 105 | 17 % | probably (no target position in the dump: upper bound) |
| Free air, clear aim line (fast ping-pong / overshoot near the target) | 81 | 13 % | no |
| Not steering: hitstun 71, super-aim 33, black hole 6 | 110 | 18 % | no |
| Refuel hops (core 24, block 15, pylon 13, shells 12, open air 32; median fuel 15) | 96 | 16 % | no (by design) |

- **Detector caveat:** stuck episodes in the well are mostly FAST. Speed quartiles are 722 / 951 / 1327 px/s, and only 35 of 218
  obstacle stucks are below 400 px/s.
  - In a constant-g radial well the "disp < 90 px in 1 s while boosting" test mostly fires on bounce ping-pong (in to a bumper face,
    kicked back, thrust in again), not on a bot pressed motionless against a face.
  - That still looks dumb on screen, but it means stuck counts on Singularity measure ping-pong.
- **Answer:** an obstacle-aware chase target addresses **~84 % of pin episodes** and **36-53 % of stuck episodes**.
  - If it removes even half of the obstacle pins, every arm clears the pin bar. Predicted A: 0.119 → ~0.07.
  - If it removes all of them: A ~0.04, B ~0.01.
  - Stuck would fall to ~0.6-0.8 per bot-stage. The remainder is stun, super-aim, refuel rhythm and free-air ping-pong.

## Spec: WK-161b obstacle-aware chase target in radial wells (short design)

**Problem:**
- In a well, `_decide` hunt and finisher aim straight at the (led) target: `_fly_aim(d.normalized())`.
- When `_attack_window_why` returns 3 (no line of sight), the bot keeps boosting to "get AROUND a blocker", but the aim still
  points through it.
- `_ascent_detour` returns early in radial frames, and `_steer`'s 240 px feeler fan only bends the aim locally. The next
  reaction tick snaps it back at the target.
- Result: the bot rests on the pylon's outer face, hops on the core, or rams the shell underside.

**Change** (InputBot only, behind `Tuning.wk161_chase_route` (default OFF) + env `GB_WK161R=0/1`):

1. **Trigger.** Hunt, finisher-pursue and the `plan` land-target branch, when `_wk161_in_well(pos)` is true and
   `_surface_along(pos, dir_to_target, dist_to_target)` hits a collider. Reuse the why == 3 ray: no extra ray when the line of
   sight is clear.
2. **Round body** (collider `has_meta("round")`, the core):
   - Let R = body_r + me._radius + `bot_route_margin` (new, 60 px).
   - The waypoint is the tangent point from pos to the circle (c, R) on side s, where s = sign(cross(pos − c, target − c)), the
     shorter arc.
   - If the bot is already inside R (touching), aim along the tangent t·s with a small outward lean (+0.25 n), the same shape
     as `_pin_avoid`.
3. **Rect body** (pylon / corner block / shell; extents from the RectangleShape2D, as `_wk135_pin_contact` already reads them):
   - Candidate waypoints: the two ends of the hit face, pushed out along the face tangent by half-length + me._radius +
     `bot_route_margin`, and off the face by me._radius + margin.
   - Pick the end with the smaller |pos→wp| + |wp→target| whose own segment pos→wp is clear (one ray). If that segment is blocked,
     try the other end. If both are blocked, fall back to today's behaviour.
   - For a pylon outer-face rest this routes over or under the pylon's end (y < 246 or y > 814) instead of through it.
4. **Hysteresis.** Latch the chosen side while the line of sight stays blocked. Re-pick only if the target moves more than 35° to
   the other side, or after `bot_route_commit_s` (0.6 s). This is a SIDE latch only: no steering after the LOS clears
   (WK-164 lesson: post-release holds multiply sharp turns).
5. **Flight.** Aim `_fly_aim(wp − pos)`. Boost as today: why == 3 already boosts. Keep `_steer`, `_pin_avoid` and `_smooth_fly` after
   it. Refuel dives and recovery are unchanged.
6. **Telemetry.** Add `route_frames`, `route_side_flips` and `route_fallbacks` to motion_health. Add `tgt` (target pos) and `route`
   (side / waypoint) to each wk161_dump record, so the next ruling doesn't need the aim-ray proxy.

**Acceptance (one batch):**
- Arms OFF/ON, Singularity 40 stages, same seeds, BR-135 ON in both.
- Pins ≤ 0.1 per bot-stage. Obstacle-class pins and stucks each down ≥ 50 %. Stuck ≤ 1.1x OFF.
- KO/min 0.90-1.15x, self-KO ≤ +3 pp, median stage within the CI.
- `route_side_flips` ≤ 1 per route episode.
- Guard slice: black_hole_brawl + twin_moons, 10 stages each (other wells, two-source orbits).
- Then a WK-93 recording on Singularity with ON, stepped through around the first pylon-outer-face approach (x ≈ 292 / 1628) and a
  core cross-chase.

**Separate, smaller follow-up:** make the stuck detector well-aware. Count an episode only when the speed is below ~400 px/s, or
report "ping-pong" (≥ 3 contacts with one face plus disp < 90 px) as its own counter. Then Singularity stuck numbers stop mixing
pinning with bounce rhythm.
