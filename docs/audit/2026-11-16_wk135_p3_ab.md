## Verdicts

| Switch | Verdict | Why |
|---|---|---|
| `refund_gates_on` (GB_WK135_GATES) | **KEEP OFF, then tune** | Fails brawl D1, D2, D3 and D6, plus the D8 bumper-arena run. All-bot sports E fails too. |
| `sports_touch_refuel` (GB_WK135_BALL) | **SHIP ON standalone (gates OFF), director's call** | In human seats it halves starvation with no regression. One all-bot goals dip is a watch item. |

### Gates: keep OFF
- **They starve bots into self-KOs, and the pacing drops below the bars.** Compared over 114 vs 114 stages with the same arena sequences:
  - **D1:** credited KO/min falls to **0.77x** (9.30 → 7.12, p = 0.001). The rotation slice falls to 0.61x.
  - **D2:** median stage rises **+24 %** (13.3 → 16.4 s; the bootstrap CIs don't overlap).
  - **D3:** self-KO share rises **+6.7 pp** (11.8 → 18.5 %, Fisher p = 0.015).
  - **Bumper Gauntlet** alone goes from **1 of 33 to 13 of 35 self-KOs** (p = 0.0006).
  - Almost every self-KO is a fuel-starved fall: 64 of 65 had ≤ 4 fuel at some point in their last 3 s.
- **D6 fails.** Bumper refunds fall only **−35 %** (bumper arenas −65 %) against a bar of −80 %.
  - Wall-hug income does fall: wall refunds go from 9.1 to 3.6 per bot-minute in the air, and in sports from 46 to 17 per CPU-minute.
- **Debug bar #16 fails.** `qa_valve_over_natural` must be 0, and it logged **5** events (one per bot-stage, on carousel, Spires,
  Drop Chamber, Singularity and Sky Ladder).
- **Ladder diagnosis.** The spec §10 row "Brawl D1 < 0.90x, no hangs" says to look at F2 recovery losses first. The data says the F1
  re-arm is the bigger bite: `refunds_blocked_rearm` is **13.8** per bot-minute vs `refunds_blocked_speed` at **2.9**. In the rotation
  slice it is 11.2 vs 1.4.
  - Knob 2 alone (wall-class F2 → 0 plus `refund_rearm_bumper_px` 400) would only treat the bumper arenas.
  - Pair it with WK-128 §10 "bots starved refuelling": change the brain to refuel off a different face, or set `refund_rearm_frac`
    0.9 → 0.75.
  - Add the carousel deck fix.
  - Then re-run this A/B.

### Ball refuel: ship ON standalone (director's call)
- Read the B arm (gates OFF, ball ON) against OFF.
- **Human seats** (3 HumanSim3 + 1 CPU L2):
  - Starved share (fuel < 10) **halves**: 0.272 → 0.138. For the human seats alone it goes 0.31 → 0.17.
  - Goals ×1.22 (n.s.), touches ×1.06, own-goal share −8.8 pp, stuck ×1.02, rest 0.
- **Invariant #17 holds.** The 1.0 s cooldown blocks about half the human touch payouts (`ball_fuel_blocked_cd` 20.5 vs
  `refunds_ball` 19.6 per human-minute). Stunned touches pay 0.
- **All-bot:**
  - Touches ×1.01 and starved ×0.91.
  - **Goals ×0.72 (p = 0.08)** is the watch item. One court drives it: CENTER, 2.25 vs 5.5 /min. The OFF bot-CENTER rate of 5.5 /min
    is high next to phase 2's gates-OFF rate on the same court and seed (4.33 /min).
- **Rally median dips.** In human seats it falls to 7.9 s, under WK-131's 8-20 s bar. The goals simply come faster. Kickoff goals
  under 4 s stay at 8.3 % (bar ≤ 10 %).
- **The full package (P3) only passes sports E in human seats.** With the ball added to the gates, human seats pass every E bar:
  goals ×1.29, touches ×1.02, starved ×0.88, own goals −17 pp. All-bot fails: goals ×0.79, touches ×0.81, starved ×1.58,
  own goals +12.8 pp. So the ball doesn't rescue the gates for CPUs. CPU income falls 1432 → 931 per minute.
