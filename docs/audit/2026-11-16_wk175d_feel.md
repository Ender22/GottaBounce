## The four questions

**Q1. Do walls still feel like part of the toybox? Yes.**
- A first bank always pays, and a carom across different faces keeps paying.
- In the 4-bot recording, posts paid 14 times against 7 toks in 30 s. Bots visibly pinball across both posts and the boxes
  (`sheet.png`), and nobody is parked on a wall.
- One bot pressed into the base of the right post for about 0.6 s (f736-754) and then left.
- In naive human play, paying banks outnumber toks (bg_kb 4:3, pp_pad 5:1). The human was never starved by a wall it hadn't just used.

**Q2. When a wall doesn't pay, do I understand why, and is it learnable rather than unfair? Better, but not there yet.**

The fatal case still happens naturally:
- In bg_pad (t 13-19 s), P1 chased a rival into the pocket beside the centre post's right face.
- It got one ding at 65 fuel, then held into the post for about 6 s, burning fuel, with toks.
- The last tok came at 0 fuel, and P1 fell into the pit (`trace` in `bg_pad.json`; the fall is in `WK-175d_pp_pad_t1.png` on the next stage).

That death is now *earned and explainable*, because the game said "no" each time. Three things still keep it from being *learnable*:

1. **Ding and tok on the same bank.** A tok on the same face 5 to 242 ms after a paying cue happened on:
   - 29 of 135 paying cues across all goats in my runs (21 %), 5 of them within 50 ms;
   - 6 of 14 in the bot recording (f77→82, f84→88, f124→135, f737→742, f801→806, f1070→1078);
   - 7 human pairs that were actually played as audio (`wall_cue_log`).

   The cause: one physical bank squashes, slides or re-touches the face in the next few ticks, or the stick is still held into it.
   - So the player hears "ding-tok" and sees flare plus grey puff (f84 shows both on Puff at once), for a bank that **did** pay.
   - This undercuts the one lesson the tok exists to teach: "you came back to this wall too soon".
   - A tok should only ever mean *you came back*. It must never mean *you're still touching it*.
2. **The visual half is lost at brawl zoom.** The camera sat at 0.55-0.57 in every 4-goat shot I took. The goat is about 45 px tall in a
   720p frame (about 70 px at 1080p).
   - **Paid flare:** a warm spark about 20-25 px at 720p (40 px at 1080p), gone by 300 ms. It's readable if you're already watching your goat
     (`WK-175d_bg_pad_paid60_2.png`, `WK-175d_bgL_paid40.png`).
     - It loses to everything else on screen: bounce rings, kill sparkle bursts, and other goats' exhaust (`WK-175d_bg_pad_paid60_1.png`, where
       a lavender burst four times its size sits beside it).
   - **Tok puff and sputter:** a soft grey disc about 15-20 px at 720p (40-50 px at 1080p in the recording), drawn on top of the squashed goat.
     It reads as "a bit of smoke", in the same visual vocabulary as ordinary jet exhaust and boost puffs (`WK-175d_bgL_tok150.png`: a CPU's normal
     exhaust puff top-left is bigger and brighter than my tok).
   - **In a two-goat cluster** it is unreadable (`WK-175d_bg_kb_tok80_0.png`, `WK-175d_hb_kb_tok100_0.png`).
   - **On Pinball Pit's grey-sepia sky** it vanishes entirely (`WK-175d_pp_pad_tok60_0.png`). The 0.52 grey puff is the same value as the
     backdrop.
   - **On Hay Bales' day sky** it shows as a small darker smudge (`WK-175d_hbL_tok40.png`).
   - **The couch-scale flip-book** (`WK-175d_rec_strip_f736-754.png`, full frames at half size): the flare is a spark at f738. The tok at
     f742-747 is a smudge I could not separate from the goat's squash without already knowing where to look.

   The builder's risk is confirmed. The cue reads in zoomed crops (WK-175c `_zoom` shots) and not in the frame a player actually sees.
3. **The audio half: what I could check.** It is present and it is human-attributed.
   - Every human cue in my runs was played: `refuel_ding` at -14.5 dB and `wall_tok` at -12.0 dB, CPU cues 6 dB under. Counts are in each
     run's `audio.wall_cue_log`.
   - In the bot-only recording, broadband RMS around each CPU cue sits roughly at the bed (median +0.4 dB over the 250 ms before it). That
     fits -6 dB CPU cues, which are meant to be background.
   - I can't judge timbre or whether it stands out at the human level. The audio is the half that will carry Q2 at a real couch. That's why
     item 1 matters more than item 2.

**Q3. Is the floor loop exactly as before? Yes.**
- In every run, `refunds_blocked_rearm` = `refunds_blocked_rearm_wall` (4=4, 10=10, 29=29, 1=1). Not one floor contact was blocked.
- Tier-floor pays continue as normal (6-14 per run), and floors are silent: no cue fired on any top face.
- The recording's paid cues at (0, 1158) and (900, 1177) are island **side** faces, which are wall-class by design.

**Q4. Sports.** Out of scope: `GB_WK175_MODES=brawl`, and the brawl-only flip leaves sports unchanged.

## FIX list (all S; no rule, refund or protected-constant change)

1. **No tok just after a pay (logic, gameplay-programmer).**
   - Swallow the tok cue for **0.30 s** (new knob `wk175c_tok_after_pay_s = 0.30`) after a PAID cue from the same goat on the same face key.
   - The contact stays blocked and pays nothing, exactly as now; only the cue is suppressed.
   - Hold-into-the-wall hugs still tok from about the 2nd-3rd knock onward (the 0.35 s tok gap already paces them).
   - Measure of done: in a re-run of this harness, paid→tok pairs under 250 ms on the same face fall from 21 % to 0 %. Hugs held over 0.6 s
     still tok.
2. **Make the visual half survive brawl zoom and every sky (Tuning plus one draw-order line).**

   | Knob | Now | Proposed | Why |
   |---|---|---|---|
   | `wk175c_puff_scale` | 1.2 | **2.0** | Puff core about 40 px at 1080p and zoom 0.56, roughly half a goat |
   | `wk175c_sputter_scale` | 1.8 | **2.8** | Puff plus sputter together span about one goat-width |
   | `wk175c_puff_s` | 0.6 | **0.7** | Still readable once the goat has bounced clear of it |
   | `wk175c_glow_scale` | 1.5 | **2.4** | Flare glow about 1 goat-width for its first 100 ms |
   | `wk175c_flare_scale` | 1.7 | **2.2** | |
   | `wk175c_burst_amount` | 14 | **20** | |

   - Two more changes alongside the table:
     - **Two-tone tok:** emit half the puff blobs charcoal `Color(0.28, 0.28, 0.32)` and half pale `Color(0.80, 0.80, 0.84)` in place of the
       single 0.52 grey. One mid-grey cannot contrast with night (about 0.2), sepia (about 0.55) and day (about 0.8) skies at once; a
       dark-and-light pair reads on all three.
     - **Draw the tok puff above the goat** (`z_index` over the Player), so it isn't hidden under the squash.
   - Better still: scale all of these by `clamp(0.85 / camera_zoom, 1.0, 1.6)` in place of the flat values above, so tight 1v1 shots don't
     balloon. At 0.56 that gives about 1.5x, the same as the table.
   - Measure of done: in a full 1280x720 frame of a 4-goat brawl, frozen 100 ms after a human tok on Bumper Gauntlet **and** on Pinball Pit, a
     reviewer can point to the cue without a crop. The paid flare must stay warm, never grey and never cyan.
3. **(P2, not blocking the flip) Anchor the cue where the eye already is.**
   - In a 4P frame a player finds their goat by its **P# tag**.
   - For human goats only, a 0.35 s tag reaction would make the cue unmissable without adding screen noise:
     - paid: the tag pops to 1.25x with a warm flash;
     - blocked: the tag gives a brief grey shake.
   - Hand to ui-ux-designer if items 1-2 don't land Q2 on their own.

**Watch item (no action):**
- Session-wide AudioManager counts were about 1 ding/s and about 0.5 tok/s across 4 goats.
- With 4 humans, all cues play at the human level. If a couch playtest ever calls the wall cues "noise", the lever is
  `wk175c_ding_gap_s` / `wk175c_tok_gap_s`, not removing the cues.
