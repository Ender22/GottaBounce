# WK-135 phase 2 A/B: BR-135 pin breaker ON vs OFF (2026-11-15, QA)

**Spec:** `docs/specs/wk135_bumper_min_bounce_2026-11-15.md` (§5 BR-135, §7.4, §9 B/C, §10 ladder) · **Data:** `tools/out/wk/WK-135_p2_ab_summary.json`

## Verdict: KEEP BR-135 ON. Next, run the pre-registered step `bot_pin_contacts` 4 -> 3 as a Singularity-only A/B, with a per-episode dump

- **Bumper arenas: works.** Pin episodes per bot-stage fall from **0.100 to 0.006** (16 to 1 episodes, p = 0.0003). Self-KO drops
  from 15.3 % to 8.9 %. That is back to phase 1's pre-kick 9.2 %, which closes the "+5.9 pp bumper-arena self-KO to watch" from phase 1.
  KO/min, median stage and post-smash survival don't move.
- **Sports: works.** Pins drop from 0.48 to 0.02 per bot-minute in rocketball and from 0.75 to 0.36 in dropshot. Rest episodes stay at
  0. Goals are within noise (see below).
- **Singularity: fails the bar in both arms, and BR-135 doesn't help there.**
  - Pin episodes per bot-stage: **0.142 (A) vs 0.108 (B)**, n.s. The spec bar is ≤ 0.1.
  - Stuck episodes rise **1.46x per bot-stage (1.55x per combat-minute)**: 121 vs 83, p = 0.002. That is a motion-health regression.
  - Only 9 of the 17 Singularity bot-stages with a pin episode also logged a pin break. The breaker only counts contacts made while
    thrusting into the face (`aim·n < −0.5`), so many Singularity pins are probably *not* thrust-into contacts (stun, black-hole pull,
    grazing thrust). Lowering `bot_pin_contacts` to 3 may therefore not close the bar.
  - Run the step anyway: it is the spec's pre-registered step (§10 row 2). Add a per-pin-episode dump (aim·n, boosting, hitstun,
    black-hole state, contact point) so the next ruling is evidence-based.
- **Don't revert.** Every overall brawl guardrail passes. Only one slice (Singularity) fails, and it fails the same way with BR-135 OFF.

## Bugs / follow-ups
1. **[P2] BR-135 raises Singularity stuck episodes 1.5x** (121 vs 83 over ~15.5 combat-min, p = 0.002). Pins there don't fall.
   - Repro: the batch above, `raw/brawl/{A,B}.singularity.*.json`, `round_stats[].motion_health[].stuck_episodes`.
   - Likely cause (unverified): the face exclusion and `_pin_avoid` slide around the round core. The exclusion is the core's whole
     radial surface, and the bot lives in that well, so the avoid steering stalls the bot. That is what trips the stuck detector.
   - Needs a WK-93 recording on Singularity (A arm) at the stuck timestamps. It wasn't recorded here: the hard resource cap allowed
     one headless batch only.
2. **[P2] Singularity bumper_pin_episodes 0.14 / bot-stage with BR-135 ON** (spec ≤ 0.1). 8 of the 17 pin bot-stages had no pin break,
   so the pins aren't caught by the "thrusting into" test.
