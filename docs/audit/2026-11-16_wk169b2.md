## Pre-registration and the one amendment

Pre-registered definitions:
- **`T2c`** (SD-free tail) = time of the first kill at or after 2-alive that leaves ≤ 1 fighter, minus the 2-alive time.
  - For a double KO it's the first death of the pair.
  - So no sudden-death time and no KO freeze is counted.
- **`E_c`** = mean of min(max(0, `T2c` − 6), 60) over forced stages.
- **Control** = A0 + A0r pooled.
- **Golden collection** = collected ÷ (collected + popped).

**The amendment:**
- **When:** logged in PREREG.json before any WK-169b-2 process ran.
- **What:** the PRIMARY gate changed from "bootstrap ratio CI90 upper < 1" to "one-sided Fisher p < 0.05".
- **Why:** a dry run of the analyzer on the already-published WK-169b logs showed the bootstrap CI collapses to [0, 0] when A1 has 0 events. That
  CI "passed" 0 / 19 vs 3 / 27, a split at Fisher p 0.19.
