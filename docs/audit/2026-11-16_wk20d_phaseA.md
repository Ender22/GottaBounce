## Method
- **Build:** HEAD **b1ca88b**, via `git archive` into a scratchpad copy:
  - the McpInteractionServer autoload removed, `config/name` renamed, `--gb-port=0`;
  - the repo's uncommitted InputBot / Tuning / SportsMode edits were not included;
  - `Tuning.gd` was never edited. The only runtime override was `circuit_session_deck=false` for lane 1, set from a boot arg.
- **Rig:**
  - headless, `--fixed-fps 120`, game time only;
  - 3 × HumanSim4: the CD4 driver plus the R1 harness's seeded RNG and SUDDEN_DEATH fix, on pad:1, pad:0 and kb;
  - 1 CPU (MIX → L2);
  - LIVES 1, ghosts ON, powerups at defaults;
  - truce, decks, carousel fix, WK-135 p3 defaults, bumper kick floor and BR-135 all as at HEAD.
- **Lane 1:** 12 seeds × 20 stages of the authored MEADOW MILE order, KILLS 999, scoreboard skipped by the harness: **240 stages** (G 36, V 156, H 48; every arena n = 12).
- **Lane 2:** 8 fresh processes, each one session of a first-to-10 match plus one REMATCH, pressed 3.0 s of game time after MATCH_END. That is **16 matches and 179 stages**, real flow, with scoreboard holds, intro and victory left on.
- **Game time:** 119.7 min (lane 1 65.1, lane 2 54.6), of which 92.3 min is combat.
  - Wall time was 18.5 min on 2 lanes. There was **one headless batch, never more than 2 Godot processes** at once.
  - Before the batch: one solo editor import pass and two short harness smoke runs.
  - I stopped only my own processes; none outlived the batch.
- **Interstitial (information only):**
  - the measured non-combat share of a real match is **28.4 %**;
  - the median stage-close-to-next-GO gap is 4.78 s;
  - at the owner-ruled 4.8 s per stage, the share would be 27.0 %.
- **Analysis:**
  - same stage metrics as the R1-R4 analyzer (`wk20d_stats.stage_rows`);
  - classes per §11.2;
  - bootstrap CIs (5,000 resamples of stages) for medians and claw-backs, Wilson for shares;
  - the R2 comparison column re-runs the same analyzer on `tools/out/wk/wk20d_batch/raw_baefff0/R2.*.json` (HumanSim3, baefff0);
  - side-edge share = human deaths through the left or right bound / all human deaths.
- **Deviations from §11.8:**
  - lane 2 produced 179 stages, not about 200 (11.2 stages per match);
  - the REMATCH press is scripted, so the victory screen is held 3 s rather than read by a person.
