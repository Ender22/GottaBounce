**Caveat the designer must weigh (calibration miss, §6.2):** HumanSim-newcomer hits the self-KO band (29 % [25-33]). It **misses the KO-rate
band**: it kills **1.11×** the CPU L2, where the band is 0.35-0.65× (CD1 ≈0.47×, CD2 ≈0.44×). No preset reached the band, including 5
off-protocol variants. On this build the bot level barely changes kill rate. So the B-batch CPU-win numbers (0-20 %) understate the
CPU dominance the CDs saw. The CD matches themselves show that dominance is **fighting strength, not scoring**: CD1 CPU 10 credited
vs best human 7, CD2 13 vs 8. Ra would not have changed either winner. On this build L1 is as lethal as L2 (0.51 credited KOs per stage
each), so Rc′ would not fix it either.

**Biggest side finding (not in the protocol, P1):** post-WK-95 **bots self-KO 36 % of their deaths** (n = 808; L3 38 %). 44 % of those
come in the first 3 s. In 4-bot stages, **59 % of first deaths happen before 3 s, and 65 % of those are self-KOs**. Two mechanisms are
logged (§5.3). This, not knockback, is what makes stages short (median 8-9 s; CD2's "pacing" gap).

## 1. Build, method, deviations

- **Build:** gameplay = `b669912` (WK-95 4135884 + WK-95e a362ad4, HOT rule ON; WK-86 in). HEAD moved to `fccf8dc` during the run, but
  those were docs-only commits, and the other builders' dirty files are UI/theme/course files. Gameplay is identical.
  `git diff src/core/Tuning.gd` SHA-1 before = after = `da39a3ee…` (empty diff): nothing persisted.
- **Where:** headless, in a scratchpad copy of the project (`McpInteractionServer` autoload removed, so port 9090 was never bound).
  `--fixed-fps 120`. The recorder, boot and HumanSim live only in that copy (their sources are saved as `.txt` under `tools/out/wk/`).
  I never used run_game/stop_game, and every process was stopped by its own exit.
- **Runtime toggles** (copy only, per spec): in Study A, even `stage_index` = arm B (`smash_sting_min_impulse 1875`,
  `hitstun_per_impulse 0.0002`), applied at STAGE_INTRO and `combat_started` and restored to 1500 / 0.00025 at every STAGE_END and at exit.
  `pu_max_on_field = 0` in A/R. The live Tuning snapshot at each stage's first smash matched its arm in **256/256** stages.
- **Roster/arenas:** default Brawl RANDOM_ALL shuffle-bag (13 arenas). None are on the explosive-arena list, so primary = all stages.
  Study A: L3, L2, L3, L4 with standard profiles (`eff_knockback_taken` = 1.0 for all, verified). Study B: `human_count 3 + bot_count 1 +
  MIX`. That gives CPU **L2** (verified), P1-P3 in group `human`, and HumanSim installed before the first BOUNCE (stage 1 is valid).
- **Game time:** 1,230 stages simulated, 235 combat-minutes (about 328 min of game time with interstitials). 0 minutes of the rendered
  game slot (the CD held it). 140 s of Movie Maker combat across 3 rendered clips. The spec's 90-min cap was a rendered-slot budget,
  and headless ran ≈4× real time.
- **Sample sizes:** spec-sized batches were run first and drive the pre-registered rows: A 40 → 56 extension, B1 40 (the extended
  size), B3 16, B2 16, all with ≥ 4 seeds. Each also has a larger **replication** (A 160, B1X 240, B3X 120, B2X 120; 4-8 seeds).
  Replication rows are reported separately. Headless runs are **not** bit-deterministic: the same seed gives the same arena order but a
  different sim, so every run is an independent sample.
- **Deviations:** (1) headless instead of the rendered slot (owner/director instruction). (2) B-cal used 24-stage rounds rather than
  6-stage ones. After the 2 allowed rounds I also ran 5 **off-protocol** sensitivity presets (S1-S3, Bcal3a/b) to test whether the KO
  band was reachable. None of them feed the rows. (3) H5 uses fuel ≥ `super_boost_min_fuel` (10, WK-95) instead of the spec's 5,
  because a press below 10 can't enter AIM on this build.

- **Calibration misses (reported per spec):** (1) the newcomer **KO band is unreachable** with the §6.2 layers on this build. Every
  preset lands at 1.0-1.2 × at n ≥ 60, and 24-32-stage rounds swing between 0.7 × and 3.5 ×. The CPU's per-stage KO rate drives that

## 7. Findings / bugs (route via producer; QA doesn't fix)

1. **[P1, bot motion / pacing] Bots self-destruct at match start and on "unreachable" recoveries.**
   - **Data** (256 4-bot stages): 36 % of bot deaths are self-KOs. 127/291 of those come in the first 3 s (earliest 0.6 s after BOUNCE),
     mostly on an empty tank (a super dumps the tank).
   - **(a) Opening attack-super whiffs.** An attack super fires within 1 s of BOUNCE in 53 % of stages, and 58 opening self-KOs follow
     a whiffed attack super.
   - **(b) `recovery_unreachable` supers at high fuel.** 131 of 422 fire at fuel ≥ 50 (seen up to 97). 74 self-KOs follow within 3 s
     (9 % of all deaths). An "unreachable" verdict at 70-97 fuel means `_nearest_ground_dx × 0.042 > fuel`, i.e. dx > 1,700-2,300 px.
     Check `_nearest_ground_dx` on the worst arenas (self-KO share: spires 70 %, the_ascent 54 %, twin_chasms 48 %, atoll 46 %;
     drop_chamber 9 %).
   - **Effect:** 59 % of 4-bot stages have their first death before 3 s, and 65 % of those are self-KOs. This is the likeliest source
     of CD2's "stages got shorter" (median 8-9 s here, vs CD1's 15 s).
   - **Repro:** `wk20a_raw_A.json`, any stage. Filter `supers[].purpose == "recovery_unreachable" && fuel >= 50` and the deaths ≤ 3 s
     later. Rendered evidence: `tools/out/rec/wk20a_humansim/` P1 (pink) fires two `recovery_unreachable` supers at fuel 38 and 20 on
     the_cliff before dying.
   - **Motion-health numbers (4-bot, per bot-stage):** stuck 0.50 ep / 0.075 s, underside 1.13 (boosted 0.205), blocked-ascent 0.020 ep,
     detours 10.3. The legacy pathologies stay healthy. This pathology is new and invisible to them.
2. **[P2, bot fidelity / Rc′] L1 is as lethal as L2 post-WK-95** (0.51 credited KOs/stage each, B1X vs B3X), and L1 self-KOs less
   (18.6 % vs 31.6 %). GDD §8 / BotProfile "L1 = funny/flailing" is still not delivered, so "MIX → L1" would not soften the CPU.
3. **[P3, instrumentation] `smash_landed` fires for hits on spawn-protected victims.** `receive_smash` returns early, but the emit
   still happens, with `sting_applied` read from the stale `_last_sting_applied`. 299 such rows (all sting 0 in this sample), and they
   land in `round_stats.smashes` as if real. QA readers must filter them. The cleaner fix is an early-out flag in the info dict.
4. **[P3, protocol] HumanSim KO-band calibration is unreachable on bot-based drivers** (§4.1). Future human-sim studies should anchor
   on the CD's device-level driver, or add an explicit "fights worse" layer (aim error on every super, not just H3 outside AIM).
