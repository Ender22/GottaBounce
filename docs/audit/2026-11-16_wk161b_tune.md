## Verdict: keep OFF. Reject the proposed tune. Next tune: margin down, not lean up.

- **The proposed tune makes things worse.** ON-tuned (`wk161b_inside_lean` 0.6 + `wk161b_route_margin` 100) has more stuck episodes
  than ON-old: **1.26x OFF (p 0.007)** against ON-old's 1.09x (p 0.34). Ping-pong is 1.29x OFF (p 0.003). ON-tuned is higher than OFF
  in 5 of 6 seeds.
- **Attribution: `route_margin` 100 is the harmful knob.**
  - margin-only: stuck **1.33x OFF (p 0.0007)**, 1.22x ON-old (p 0.017). KO/min 0.86x. Longest stage 131 s.
  - Refuel-hop stuck records double (42 → 86). A wider route burns fuel, and the bot then refuels by hopping on the core.
- **`inside_lean` 0.6 does what it targets, but the stucks just move.**
  - lean-only: routed stucks at the core fall 31 → 18, but routed stucks on bumper floors rise 29 → 44.
  - Net: 1.04x ON-old (p 0.67). Neutral, not a fix.
- **ON-old is borderline on this build, but it is not shippable.**
  - It passes nominally on stuck (1.09x), KO/min (0.93x), self-KO (−0.6 pp), pins (0.046) and obstacle-class pins (−70 %).
  - It **fails** obstacle-class stucks: +29 % by the aim proxy and +33 % for "either", against a bar of −50 %.
  - It **fails** median stage: 33.0 s, outside OFF's CI of [24.1, 31.4].
  - Pooled with the acceptance batch, ON-old's stuck excess is **1.19x (p 0.008)**. The acceptance batch said 1.35x and this batch says
    1.09x, so the truth is somewhere between, and above the bar.
- **Next tune (for the programmer):**
  1. Margin went 60 → 100 and stuck went up monotonically. Try **`wk161b_route_margin` 30-40** as one arm.
  2. Look at **refuel-on-core**. A routed bot that runs low on fuel ends up hopping on the core top. The recording shows this (f511-590),
     and refuel-hop stucks rise in every arm that raises margin or lean.
  3. **Lean: leave it at 0.25.** Raising it only trades core contacts for rect contacts.
  4. The "obstacle-class stucks −50 %" bar probably cannot be met by routing in its current form. Every routed bot has a blocked target line
     by definition, so `tgt_blocked` stucks rise mechanically. The designer should restate this bar as "routed stucks per route-second", or drop it.

## WK-93 recording: ON-old (the best ON arm on the stuck bar), Singularity, 4 x L3, 30 s, seed 17

- **How it was made.** `record_match.ps1` was run from the HEAD scratch copy with `GB_WK161B=1`. The recorder cannot take `tune_` args,
  but ON-old uses the current knobs, so no edit was needed. Routing was live: `route_frames` per bot were 235, 490, 272 and 544, with
  0 side flips.
- **What was looked at.** `sheet.png`, `sheet_overlay.png` and `traj.png`, plus step-through montages in the recording directory:
  - `steps_f62-106_pylon_route.png`
  - `steps_f476-592_core_top.png`
  - `steps_f600-1120_stage2.png`
- **Pylon outer face, f62-106. Routing works.** Puff starts against the right pylon's outer face (x 1631-1644, vx flipping ±170 over
  f61-77, a 0.7 s spawn pin). The route goes live at f81 with waypoint (1688, 186). The bot climbs past the pylon end and over the top-right
  block by f100, then joins the fight. It does not slide back and does not dither. This is the spec's "first pylon outer-face approach"
  case, and it is solved.
- **Core top, f471-560. Ping-pong, but not caused by routing.** Pogo has 0 fuel and bounces vertically between the core top (y ≈ 410) and
  the underside of the top shell (y ≈ −10): 5 vy flips in 2 s, boost off. With no fuel the bot cannot steer, so this is an empty-tank pinball,
  not routing. It reads as "helpless", which is fair. Puff smashes it out at f567 and it is KO'd off the top at f591.
- **Core top, f511-590. Refuel hug.** Puff hops in place on top of the core at fuel 0-20 (y 305-418, 10 vy flips in 2 s) and refuels to 95
  by f561. This is the "refuel-hop stuck" class that margin and lean inflate in the batch. Visually it is a small, jittery bob on the core rim.
  It is not terrible, but it is the motion behind the metric.
- **Bottom exit, f1051-1081. Credited, fine.** Butch leaves through the bottom at x 69, at 2600 px/s straight down, after Ziggy's
  attack-dump super at f1051.
- **What did not appear:**
  - No routed bot orbiting the core.
  - No bot pinned on a pylon or block for more than 1 s after spawn.
  - No side dither.
  - No bottom self-exit: 4 KOs, all credited. The pit self-KO mechanism therefore stays uncaptured, although on HEAD it is no longer
    ON-specific.
- **Motion health, stage 1:** stuck 0 / 1 / 2 / 1, underside 14 / 3 / 3 / 4, route episodes 3 / 2 / 5 / 8, `route_max_s` 2.83.
- Stage 1 combat is clustered round the core. That is how this arena plays.
