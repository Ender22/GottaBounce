# WK-161b acceptance batch: chase routing in gravity wells, OFF vs ON (2026-11-16, QA)

**Spec:** `docs/audit/2026-11-16_wk161_ab.md` §Spec · **Build:** de3553c (switch `wk161b_chase_route`, env `GB_WK161B`)
· **Data:** `tools/out/wk/WK-161b_accept_summary.json` · **Raw JSON, logs, harness, classified dump:** `tools/out/wk/wk161b_accept/`
(`raw/*.json`, `jobs.py`, `st161b.py`, `st161b_full.json`)

## Verdict: keep OFF and tune. Do not ship ON as built.

- **What works.**
  - Pins clear the bar: 0.100 → **0.062** per bot-stage.
  - Obstacle-class pins fall **60 %** (15 → 6).
  - The real "pressed against a face" pathology nearly disappears: `well_pinned_episodes` 11 → 3 (0.27x, p = 0.057), and pin
    breaks halve (p = 0.015).
  - Side flips are 0.11 per route episode, against a bar of ≤ 1, and routing almost never falls back (0.001 per episode).
- **What fails.**
  - **Stuck is 1.35x OFF** (p = 0.004; higher in all 6 seeds). All of the rise is fast ping-pong: `well_pingpong` is 1.43x
    (p = 0.0008). Refuel-hop stucks rise from 21 to 56.
  - **Obstacle-class stucks do not fall** (−8 % by the aim proxy, +4 % by the target line; the bar is −50 %).
  - **KO/min is 0.84x** (p = 0.12).
  - **Self-KO rises +3.3 pp** (4 → 10 of 180 deaths, Fisher p = 0.17). **7 of the 10 ON self-KOs are bottom "pit" exits; OFF has
    none.**
- **Routing trades pinning for bouncing.** A routed bot that skirts the core (a bumper, body_r 120, g 1000) gets kicked off at
  every contact. It no longer sits on a face, but it still makes no progress, and the stage drags.
- **Tune knob, in this order:**
  1. **`wk161b_inside_lean` 0.25 → ~0.6.** Routed bots touch the core in 27 of 83 routed stuck records, and 3 of 5 routed pins are
     at the core.
  2. Then **`wk161b_route_margin` 60 → ~100** as a second arm.
  - Re-run this exact batch (same seeds), then make the owed WK-93 recording.
  - The bottom-exit self-KOs have no mechanism in the data yet (see Bugs). The recording must show at least one of them before
    anyone ships ON.

  0 SCRIPT ERRORs, 0 hangs, 20/20 runs complete.

## Where the costs come from (dump with the new `tgt` and `route` fields)

- **Remaining ON pins (10):**
  - 5 have a live route.
  - 2 are pylon outer-face slides toward the waypoint past the pylon end. For example, x = 1628, y = 728 heading to (1688, 874):
    the routing is working, just slowly.
  - 3 are core contacts inside the tangent band. The aim is the tangent plus a 0.25 outward lean, and gravity (1000) holds the bot on
    the bumper core.
  - The other 5 are refuel hops or not steering.
  - The OFF signature is gone: 10 of OFF's 19 pins are hunt bots resting on the pylon's outer face with no route.
- **Routed stucks (83 of 223 ON):**
  - Contact class: core 27, bumper floor 20, bumper ceiling 9.
  - Median speed: 970 px/s.
  - In 45 of them the aim line is already clear. The bot is flying the waypoint and bouncing, which is ping-pong, not pinning.
- **Refuel-hop stucks 21 → 56:**
  - Route is not live in any of them. Core contacts 13 → 26.
  - Fuel economy per unit time is unchanged (starved share 36.3 % vs 36.4 %).
  - Most likely, routed bots spend more time hugging the core and then refuel on it. This is an inference: the dump has no
    position history.
- **Self-KOs:**
  - Every ON bot-stage routes at least once, so "the victim routed this stage" carries no information.
  - All 7 pit self-KOs leave through the bottom blast line (y > 1900, inside the well, where gravity points up) at |vx| 500-1400 px/s,
    with no recent hit.
  - Pit deaths in total: OFF 50 (all credited), ON 43 (36 credited, 7 self). Under ON, some bottom exits happen without an enemy
    touch.
  - Mechanism not identified. Candidates: a bumper or shell kick while routing round the bottom shell or the corner blocks, or an
    overshoot on a block-end waypoint (`wk161b_bounds_inset` 150 still allows waypoints at y ≤ 1750).

## Bugs / follow-ups

   - Stuck is 1.35x OFF (p 0.004) and `well_pingpong` 1.43x (p 0.0008), while `well_pinned` falls 73 %.
   - Repro: `tools/out/wk/wk161b_accept/st161b_full.json` → `dump_records["ON.singularity"]` where `route.live` and `face == "core"`
     (27 records).
   - Tune first: `wk161b_inside_lean` 0.25 → ~0.6. Then `wk161b_route_margin` 60 → ~100.
2. **[P2] WK-161b ON: new bottom-exit self-KOs on Singularity.**
   - 7 of 10 ON self-KOs are "pit" exits, against 0 under OFF. twin_moons ON has 3 bottom self-KOs.
   - Repro: `levels.singularity.ON.self_ko_list` in `st161b_full.json`, e.g. seed 23 stage 9 t 9.78 s (victim seat 1, vx 1424) and
     seed 29 stage 8 t 2.36 s.
   - Not reproducible by seed in isolation, because arms diverge after the first KO. Needs the WK-93 recording with ON, stepped
     through bottom exits.
3. **[P3] KO/min 0.84x and a longer stage tail under ON** (mean +14 %, max 101.6 s, 5 stages > 60 s against 3). This is likely the
   same cause as #1.
4. **[P3] twin_moons: route side dither** (6 flips in one episode). Watch `route_flip_max` in the retune batch.
5. **Owed:** the WK-93 Singularity recording (spec: first pylon outer-face approach at x ≈ 292 / 1628 and a core cross-chase), plus
   bottom-exit moments. `record_match.ps1` inherits `GB_WK161B`.
6. **Keep:** the new telemetry. The `well_pinned` / `well_pingpong` split and the dump's `tgt` and `route` fields made this ruling
   possible without the aim-ray proxy.
