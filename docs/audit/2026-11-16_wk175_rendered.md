**Build:** the working tree on `weekend/polish` at `e2cf8cc`, plus other lanes' uncommitted WK-169c files (they parsed: 0 SCRIPT

**Arms:**

## 1. sports_court wall hug (Rocketball)

**Setup:**
- `rocketball` match on `levels/sports_court.json`, 1 human + 1 CPU. The CPU is parked frozen at (2300, 1250).
- P1 is placed at (345, 430) beside the upper-left wall face (x 290, y 210-650) with 50 fuel.
- Real keyboard input: **A + SPACE held**. Two pilots:
  - **"hold":** W is pressed whenever the goat is below y 430, i.e. a player holding FLY into the wall and nudging up to stay on it.
    This is the spec case.
  - **"straight":** A + SPACE only.

**WR, "hold"** (`WK-175_r1_WR_kbhold.json`; images `_t0..t4.png` and `_zoom.png`):
- First touch at 0.067 s pays +20 (50 → 68.1).
- Then 24 wall contacts on the same face, all blocked (`refunds_blocked_rearm_wall` 24, `refunds_wall_hug` 0).
- Fuel drains at −28.6/s: 41.7 @1 s, 13.1 @2 s, 0 @2.46 s. **Empty 2.39 s after first touch** (bar ≤ 3.0).
- The goat then **drops** (y 434 → 978 by 3.25 s).
- After the empty drop it gets fresh-face pays: the lower goal-post top (floor class) and the goal-mouth net back (a new wall).
  The headless probe saw the same.

**OFF, "hold"** (`WK-175_r1_OFF_kbhold*`): 57 refunds in 5 s (one every ~0.085 s, `refunds_wall_hug` 56). The tank sits at 97.9-100
the whole time and the goat stays glued to the wall at y ≈ 385-435.

**Screenshots LOOKED at:**

  - OFF: the goat is flattened against the post at the same height in every frame. That is the hover farm.

  - OFF: 57 refunds.
  - WR P1 card: ~45 % at 0 s, small at 1 s, a sliver at 2 s, empty with a grey flame at 3 s.
  - OFF card: full at 1-4 s.

**WR, "straight"** (A + SPACE only; `WK-175_r1_WR_kbstraight*`):

- It empties at 3.16 s, **3.09 s after first touch**: over the 3.0 s bar only because a second, distinct face paid. The rule is
  working as written.

## Findings

**F1 [Medium, UX, pre-existing; route to ui-ux-designer] Rocketball has no per-player fuel gauge.**
- SportsHUD shows team scores only (`src/ui/SportsHUD.gd`: "SPORTS shows TEAM scores (polled), not per-player cards"), and there
  is no fuel display on the goat.
- Spec §1 P5 says "walls refuel once" is learnable from the gauge. In Rocketball, the mode the CD flagged as most at risk, a
  player can't see the +20 or the drain. They only notice that the jetpack dies.
- Under WR fuel state matters more in sports, because sports was where wall hovering was the refuel.
- Repro: start Rocketball, look at the HUD (`WK-175_r1_court_start.png`).
- Suggest a PlayerCard-style gauge, the WK-36 bubble fuel ring on-body, or the refuel ding (WK-128 §7.5) before or with the ship.

**F2 [Low-Med, design; route to game-designer as WK-175b evidence] Goal-mouth floor dribble = keeper camp in Rocketball.**
- What happens: with A + SPACE held from the upper-left wall, the empty goat slides into its own goal mouth (x 140-290, y 650-950).
  From 3.25 s to 5 s it sits there at 15-35 fuel.
- Mechanism: it bounces on the floor-class top of the lower goal post (y 950, `@783`, always pays) about every 0.7 s. That gives
  +20 per bounce against the 28.6/s drain, so net ≈ 0 and it can stay indefinitely.
- Meanwhile it presses the net back, which does not re-pay (blocked).
- Under OFF the same input pins at 100 on the net back instead.
- This is the spec's accepted §1 floor-dribble residual, but here it is not "exposed at the bottom of the arena": it's a goalkeeper
  spot inside its own net.
- Evidence: `WK-175_r1_WR_kbstraight.json` (refunds at 3.26 / 3.43 / 4.12 / 4.83 s, all `refunds_bumper_floor`) and
  `_zoom.png` (t = 3-4 s: the goat squashed inside its own net).
- Not a WR blocker: OFF is worse. It is worth a look before anyone calls sports "hover-free".

**F3 [Low, motion pathology, pre-existing; WK-168 motion-health bucket] Stage-opening post bonk on Bumper Gauntlet.**
- What happens: at every stage start (seed 7), Ziggy (spawn on island B) and Puff (island C) boost straight left toward the island-A
  scrum and hit the RIGHT face of POST-L / POST-R at y ≈ 920-980 (the lower half of the post).
- They buzz 5-7 contacts in 0.40-0.47 s with the squash sprite, then climb over the post.
- WR frames: f78-97 (both), f660-673 (Puff), f1226-1239 (both). OFF: f78-95 (both).
- Under WR this costs them ~15 fuel instead of being free; it doesn't last longer.
- It reads as a "bonk", not a pinball carom. TowerFall-bar bots should route over the post before reaching it.
- Repro: `powershell -File tools/record_match.ps1 -Name x -Arena bumper_gauntlet -Levels 3,3,3,3 -Seconds 30 -Seed 7`, then frames
  76-114. Evidence: `WK-175_rec_{WR,OFF}_gauntlet_opening_postbonk_f76-114.png`.

**Note (not a bug):** a straight A + SPACE hold slides the goat down a 440 px court wall in ~1.9 s (`refund_gates` independent), as
the WK-175 lessons already record. The headless P2 probe needs its altitude-hold pilot for this reason.

## Feel notes
- **WR on the court wall feels like the spec's fantasy.** The first bank pays (sputter-free), then leaning on the wall is visibly a
  losing move: the flame dies and the goat falls into the goal mouth in ~2.5 s.
- **OFF is the camp.** A goat pancaked on a post at full tank forever, which reads as broken even in stills.
- **Floors are untouched.** The no-input trampoline loop is tick-identical, so the "bounce = fuel" rhythm (one +20 every 1.16 s)
  is exactly as before.
- **Bumper Gauntlet under WR still plays as pinball.** Goats carom off posts and blocks constantly; only same-face repeats go
  unpaid.
- **Pacing in this one sample:** WR played 3 stages / 7 KOs in 30 s against OFF's 1 stage / 2 KOs. This matches the A/B
  direction (KO/min 1.10x), but the sample is n = 1 and chaotic after the first KO.
