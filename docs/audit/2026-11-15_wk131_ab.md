- **§5.4 step 1:** A1 at T 3.0 fails only because of gate-drop goals. That triggers **step 2:** run T 3.5 and apply rule 1 to it.
  - T 3.5 passes every rule-1 line: kickoff goals ≤ 10 %, gate-drop ≤ 10 %, 0 goals before open, median rally inside 8-20 s.
- **LIVE BALL is out:** it fails rule 1 (19.2 %), so step 3 never applies.
- **The formation is out:** it does nothing (29.9 % vs 32.4 %). The human in the keeper slot was within 400 px of their own mouth at

  guardrail (below).
- **Does T 3.5 just move the cliff 0.5 s later?** No.
  - The "< 4 s" line is partly met by construction at T 3.5, because only [3.5, 4.0) can count. So judge it on the intent metric, which

  - At T 3.0 the kickoff scramble is still live when the gates open: 5 of the first 11 gate-drop goals were own goals in the swarm. By

## Bugs / findings
1. **MED (guardrail fail): QA-131-2, the CPU supporter isn't home when the gates open.**
   - Spec §6 bar: the CPU supporter is within 300 px of its own mouth at T in ≥ 50 % of kickoffs.
   - Measured: 12.3 % at T 3.0 (median 937 px away) and 33.3 % at T 3.5 (617 px away). Limiting to kickoffs where the CPU is the
     supporter (rank ≥ 1): 12 % and 43 %.
   - The H5 window (`BOT_GATE_RETURN_LEAD_S` 0.8 → 2.2 s / 2.7 s) doesn't get an L2 bot home from a 2v2 kickoff clash.
   - This is not a rule blocker, because the gate-drop goals come from the human swarm. File it for lane S (WK-108b is the natural home)
     with this number as its Accept.
   - Repro: `wk131_stats.py` column `bot_supporter_within300_at_T` on `raw_71e55ca/A1*.json`. Positions are in each stage's
     `rec.snaps.open`.
2. **LOW: QA-131-1, the GoalZone latch never reads `_gated`.**
   - `GoalZone.set_gated()` stores `_gated` (L130-131), but the latch poll (L147, `if not scored and _live:`) never checks it.
   - That means the spec §4.3 backup ("the zone doesn't latch while its gate is closed") isn't implemented. Only the collider stops a
     gated goal.
   - No live symptom: 0 ball-in-gated-mouth ticks and 0 goals before open across 248 gated kickoffs (A1 + A1b + A2 + LH1). It is
     defence in depth only.
   - Fix: `if not scored and _live and not _gated:`.
3. **INFO: WK-116 is not met (see below).** CENTER also produced a 63.9 s rally (A1b) and a 51.8 s rally (A3).
