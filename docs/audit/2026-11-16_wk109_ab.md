Walking the §7.4 decision rule:
- **Rows 1 and 2:** neither arm passes M1-M4.
- **Row 3** (only M1 fails, at 10-15 %): doesn't apply. A2 fails M1 at 29.6 % and also fails M2 and the M3 ratio.
- **Row 4** (G1 low): doesn't apply. Human-style goals/min are A1 0.97x and A2 1.02x.
- So **no continuation step ran.** The batch's decision gate was released with `NOCONT`.
- **Row 5:** strictly, G5 fails in some block of every ON arm (see G5 below), so the rule also says KEEP OFF. Either way the
  outcome is the same.

**Why the bars are out of reach.** The spec's speed model doesn't match the build:
- §1 reasons from a striker at ≤ 800 px/s (the boost cap) hitting a ball at rest.
- Measured at the moment of a normal touch, the striker is moving at a median of **~1000 px/s** (p75 ~1400, p90 ~1750). Bounce-charged
  flight, dives and supers that have bled off all exceed the boost cap.
- The ball is already moving at a median of **~1400 px/s** (60 % of touches meet a ball faster than 1200).
- Even the from-rest part of the touch (no v_in) gives a median of ~2050 with the 350 floor.
- The tanh knee squeezes only pre-knee speeds between 1600 and ~2700. A2's implied pre-knee speed has a median of 2019, p75 2820 and
  p90 3635. Everything above 2683 still lands at ≥ 2300.
- **Static counterfactual** (A2's touches pushed through other curves; it ignores feedback):

  | Knee | Asymptote | ≥ 2300 | Median | Power / median |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | 1400 (the row-3 continuation) | 2400 | 24.5 % | — | — |
  | 800 | 2400 | 12.2 % | — | — |
  | 1000-1400 | lowered to 2000-2200 | 0 % (by construction) | 1762-1908 | 1.47-1.59 |

  So M2 and the 1.75 ratio can't be reached by reshaping the curve. The input speeds have to come down.

## 9. Follow-ups

1. **Game-designer (WK-109 redesign).** Rebase §1/§2 on the measured touch population: striker ~1000 px/s median, ball \|v_in\|
   ~1400.
   - Lever options. **None has been tested here**, and the first two touch PROTECTED constants, so each needs a ruling:
     - carry less of `v_in` through a touch (the ball "absorbs" some of the incoming speed);
     - cap the striker-speed term (e.g. `min(|v|, 800)`, which is what §1 implicitly assumed);
     - or accept a lower bar: keep the knee, re-scope M1 to "≤ 25 %", and keep M2 as is.
   - Reshaping the knee or its asymptote alone cannot reach M2 or the 1.75 ratio (static counterfactual above).
   - Also rule on whether "a normal touch blunts the cannon" should stand. It blunts 56-60 % of power shots.
2. **QA watch (if WK-109 returns).** Gate-drop goals on THE BOX all-bot, A2 12/62 vs 2/71. All are DRIVE touches; the cause is unknown.
3. **Process note.** Same-seed arms are not paired (lesson WK-161 / WK-116 §3). Dropshot A0 vs its own repeat diverges at touch 24. Any
   "identical" bar needs an A0-repeat control, as this batch had.
