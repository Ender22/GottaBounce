## Re-queue recommendation (WK-169b-2, one headless slot)

**1. Power.**
- At ~52 forced stages per arm, the log-ratio SE of `E` is about 0.32.
- Detecting a true 0.70 with CI90 upper < 1 at 80 % power needs SE ≈ 0.14: **about 250 forced stages per arm.**
- **Run A0, A0r and A1 only (about 750 stages).** That's about 38 processes × 20 stages, about 30-35 min wall on 2 lanes.
- Drop A2 and A3 from lane T:
  - L2 already misses S5 (≈ 0.91×) and its puff rate (×1.65);
  - A3 fails G3.

**2. Pre-register the fixes this batch exposed:**
- **(a)** S4 on **SD-free** tails. SD time counts in T2, but by design the showdown can't act on it.
  - Exploratory here: A1 / pooled-control `E` 0.82 [0.51, 1.41].
- **(b)** Golden collection as a share of **resolved** drops (collected + expired). Drops still live at the stage end are excluded.
- **(c)** A0 and A0r pooled as the control, with A0r-vs-A0 still reported as P2.
- **(d)** A survival read: P(T2 > 20 | T2 > 6).

**3. Lane N as guardrail-only.** S1s and S3 come from the §10.6.3 projection plus the touchable-component metric. Lane N's raw S1s can't
resolve −8 % at about 40-75 stages per arm: the identical-code part moved 35-39 % here.

**4. Design input (game-designer).**
- Even the S4 pass bar (`E` 0.70) projects to only −30 % on waits > 15 s. S3's −40 % needs `E` ≲ 0.62.
- Meeting both S1s and S3 means L1 must beat its own S4 bar, or the bars should be re-pre-registered around the projection.
- G9 is borderline: pooled holder-win is 88 %. The ready knob is `showdown_drop_dur` 6 → 4.

## Findings outside the A/B (no item filed; for the producer)

**1. A chained double-KO revives more than two goats** (`PartyMatch._recent_dead`).
- **Cause:** every elimination within `double_ko_window` (0.5 s) of the *previous* one joins the group. A 3-death chain where each death is
  < 0.5 s after the last revives all three, or all four, into "sudden death".
- **Spec says otherwise:** spec §5 and the code comment say "both tied players".
- **Frequency:**
  - lane T made it common: 7 / 240 stages, because the force kills 2 goats at once;
  - in natural play it needs 3 eliminations inside about 1 s with nobody left.
- **Repro:** lane T, e.g. `raw_6af686c/T.A0.201.json`, meadow_spine. Seats 2 and 3 were forced out at 3.007 s, seat 1 died at 3.26 s and seat 0
  at 3.48 s, and all four revived. The SD ran 31 s.
- **Severity:** low (rare naturally). It's a rules-correctness bug.

**2. Telemetry note.**
- `tail.t2_s` includes SD time and the 0.38 s KO freeze.
- `spectator` is correct: it matches an independent re-derivation to 0.0005 s on every non-chained stage.
