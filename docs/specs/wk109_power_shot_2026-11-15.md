## 0. The player fantasy

> "I lined up behind the ball, fired the super, and it left like a cannon. A white-hot streak, a BOOM over the crowd, and the keeper
> never moved." The other 95 % of the time: "I chased it down, cushioned it out of the air, tapped it to my partner and drove it at the
> net."

Today every touch is loud. A boosted header leaves at about 2030 px/s, a quarter to a third of all touches end within 100 px/s of the
2400 cap, and the super, the game's signature verb, tops out at that same 2400 (1.3-1.5x a normal touch). There is no soft touch and no
cannon. This spec opens up both ends: **softer at the bottom, a squeezed top for normal touches, and a separate band above the normal
cap that only a fired super can reach.**

### Core verbs exercised (GAME_ESSENCE obligation)

| Verb | How this spec uses it | How the build is measured to be USING it |
|---|---|---|
| **SUPER-SMASH** | The super header becomes the only way above the normal cap: the POWER SHOT | power-touch exit speed / median normal ≥ 1.75 (today ~1.4); power-shot goals reported per arm |
| **FLY / BOOST** | Drive strength still scales with flight speed (`BALL_DRIVE_GAIN` 0.9 is PROTECTED); only the top is squeezed | median normal touch 1300-1600 px/s; human boost share ±5 pt of OFF |
| **BOUNCE** | Untouched (restitution, floor, walls) | ball parked > 0.5 s = 0 episodes |
| **RECOVER** | n/a in sports | n/a |

## 1. Evidence (why, and the numbers this spec reasons from)

- **SP-07 (WK-98, pre-WK-95):** super touches 1.5 % of Rocketball touches. They leave at 2400 = 1.3-1.5x the median normal touch
  (1630-1830). 24-33 % of normal touches land within 100 px/s of the cap.
- **WK-95 made it worse:** boost cap 760 -> 800, so a full-boost touch is now (500 + 0.9 x 800) / 0.6 = **2033 px/s from rest**.
- **Own goals sit at the cap.** WK-108b: 37 of 41 all-bot CPU own goals came from a bot above 800 px/s, 36 boosting. Those touches
  leave at the cap: in `raw_60ce151/OFF_Cb.*` most goal-touch `bv` values are exactly 2400, and the rest are 1145-2357. A bad line-up
  at full boost is a cannon into your own net, and it is no faster than a deliberate super.
- **How touches work today** (`Ball._drive_from`): `v_out = limit(v_in + dir x mag / 0.6, 2400)`, where `mag = 500 + 0.9 x |v_player|`,
  doubled if the striker is super-active OR in hitstun. The ball has no body collision with goats. It only takes this impulse, so a
  "block" is a counter-impulse, not a wall. A super striker (|v| 2000-3000) produces `mag / 0.6` ≈ 7,000-10,000 px/s, so every super
  touch saturates the cap.
- **The super identity is ruled** (DECISIONS WK-95): un-charged, drain-all, constant impulse 2800. The power shot must inherit that
  identity. It is a constant cannon, not a charge meter.

## 2. The design: three tiers, one meaning each

| Tier | Who gets it | Exit speed | Reads as |
|---|---|---|---|
| **SOFT** (cushion / dribble / tap) | any non-power touch whose exit < 1200 px/s | 583-1200 | a light "tok", a small puff. The ball goes where you nudged it |
| **DRIVE** (header / boosted strike) | any non-power touch, 1200 to the normal cap | 1200-2400, squeezed above the knee (§2.2) | the "thwock" today, pitched and leveled by impact |
| **POWER SHOT** | a **super-active striker at ≥ 900 px/s who is not in hitstun** | **exactly 2800** | cannon: hot streak, BOOM on the Stingers bus, "POWER SHOT!" pop, rumble + shake (existing) |

SOFT and DRIVE are measurement and audio labels on one continuous formula. POWER is the only separate code path.

### 2.1 Floor: `touch impulse` 500 -> 350
- From rest: 350 / 0.6 = **583 px/s**. That still clears the v5 §2 "a dead-rest ball takes ≥ 500 px/s from a slow touch" rule, so
  the touch floor still moves the ball (PROTECT).
- A full-boost touch from rest: (350 + 720) / 0.6 = **1783** (was 2033). A goat drifting at 300 px/s: **1033** (was 1283).
- **The cushion (emergent, wanted):** a slow goat meeting an incoming ball now takes most of its speed off instead of sending it back
  at 1300+. The ball drops onto the bouncy floor and never parks (Pillar 2 holds). That is first-touch control, the low-end dynamic
  range SP-07 says is missing.
- `sports_gate_spit_speed` 450 stays below the new floor (583), so the gate spit is still "not a touch" (its Tuning comment cites 833.
  Update the comment, not the value).

### 2.2 The squeezed top for normal touches: a soft knee at 1600
For a non-power touch, after `v_in + dv` and before the normal cap:

```
raw = |v_in + dv|
if knee > 0 and raw > knee:  out = knee + (cap - knee) * tanh((raw - knee) / (cap - knee))   # direction unchanged
```

With knee 1600 and cap 2400: raw 1800 -> 1796 · 2000 -> 1970 · 2200 -> 2108 · 2400 -> 2209 · 2800 -> 2325 · 3400 -> 2382.
- **Monotonic:** a harder hit is always faster, so "fly faster, hit harder" survives. Below 1600 it is the identity.
- **Why it's needed on top of the trim:** the trim alone only moves `dv` by 250 px/s. Every touch whose pre-cap speed was above
  about 2650 still pins to the cap, and that is the chase-and-re-hit population. The knee is what gets the near-cap share under 10 %.
  The A/B tests trim alone (A1) against trim + knee (A2), so the simpler fix ships if it is enough (§7.4).
- The knee is applied **at the touch only**. Gravity can still pull a falling ball above 1600. The 2400 hard cap in
  `_integrate_forces` is unchanged, as are hazards (`receive_hazard_impulse`), gates and every other reader of the normal cap.

### 2.3 The POWER SHOT
- **Trigger:** `p.is_super_active() and not p.in_hitstun() and p.velocity.length() >= Tuning.smash_impact_min` (900). This is the same
  eligibility a super needs to smash in Brawl (INV-1). A super whose goat has already bled to 600 px/s off a wall is a DRIVE touch.
  One rule for "your super is hot" everywhere.
- **Exit:** `linear_velocity = (v_in + dv).normalized() * wk109_power_speed` (2800). The direction is today's formula (blend of away
  and travel). The speed is **constant**: the cannon always looks and sounds the same, like the un-charged constant-impulse super that
  produced it. `dv` from a super striker is always more than 4,000 px/s, so `v_in + dv` can't approach zero.
- **Power window on the ball:** the ball's cap is raised to 2800 for `wk109_power_hold_s` (0.8 s), then eases back to 2400 over
  `wk109_power_decay_s` (0.3 s). That is a gentle slow-down, never a snap. It ends **immediately** on:
  - any non-power player touch. The defender's touch "blunts" the shot, and the trail dies on that frame, which is readable;
  - a hazard punt (hazards are not the player's verb);
  - STAGE_END.

  It **survives surface bounces**: a banked power shot is the highlight. Restitution 0.85 usually drops it below the trail threshold
  anyway, so the trail stays honest (§3.1).
- **Why 2800, not the backlog's ~3200:**
  1. **= `super_boost_impulse_full`.** The ball leaves at the super's own speed, so the fantasy maps 1:1.
  2. **< player `max_speed` 3000.** The v5 rule "even a power shot stays chaseable" still holds, because a super-launched flier can
     match it.
  3. **The kickoff gate's 2-tick invariant still holds without touching WK-131.** 2800 / 120 Hz x 2 = 46.7 px < 48 px collider
     (`sports_gate_thickness` comment). At 3200 it would be 53 px, so the gates would have to change, and "gates unchanged" is a bar.
  4. **Range:** 2800 / ~1500 median normal ≈ **1.9x**, and 1.2-1.35x over the squeezed DRIVE top (2100-2350). That's enough to read,
     with the streak doing the rest. At 1080p whole-court zoom (~0.65) it is ~30 screen px per frame against a ~78 px ball, so it is
     trackable (Pillar 5).
  5. **Flatter flight:** from midfield to the mouth (1160 px) it takes 0.41 s and drops ~135 px under g 1600 (2400 gives 0.48 s and
     ~185 px). The power shot flies on a line, so aim is rewarded.
- **Hitstun strikers are not POWER.** A goat smashed into the ball keeps the x2 `BALL_SMASH_MULT` (pinball comedy stays, Pillar 3),
  but its output goes through the knee and the normal cap. No streak, no pop, no power cue. **Readability rule: the POWER SHOT cue means
  "someone chose to fire a super into this ball."** A tumbling victim never earns it. In the ON arm this also narrows `is_power` on
  `Ball.touched` and `EventBus.ball_struck` to POWER only. The end-screen POWER stat, `AudioManager` and `Juice` read it unchanged and
  inherit the new meaning (no `Juice.gd` edit).
- **Super vs super:** a keeper's own super on an incoming power shot is itself a POWER touch, so the ball goes back at 2800. A normal
  keeper touch only blunts it (a full-boost touch takes ~1800-2500 off). "Only a super stops a super" is a readable, essence-true rule.
  The A/B watches whether it makes power shots too hard to defend (goals/min bar, §7.3).

### 2.4 Rejected
- **A charged shot / charge window.** It contradicts the ruled un-charged, constant-impulse super (DECISIONS WK-95). The super-aim
  phase (arrow, aim time) is already the defender's telegraph. Nothing new is needed.
- **Power cap 3200.** It breaks the chaseable rule and the gate 2-tick margin (above).
- **Lowering the normal cap below 2400.** It would move hazard punts and the gate thickness rationale, and the knee gets the range
  without it.
- **Hitstun = POWER.** Readability (above).
- **Dropshot in this pass.** It is the healthiest mode in the game (WK-98 §2.9, PROTECT), its ball has its own 1900 cap and float, and
  the A/B budget is one variable at a time. If Rocketball ships ON, a separate follow-up asks whether a POWER SPIKE belongs there.
- **Hit-stop:** WK-90 (blocked).

## 3. Readability: Rule of Three without hit-stop (FEEL §5)

| Channel | SOFT | DRIVE | POWER SHOT |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Visual** | existing squash / flash / sparks, scaled by impact (smaller now) | existing, unchanged | existing gold sparks + **streak trail** (§3.1) + **"POWER SHOT!" pop** (§3.2) |
| **Audio** | `ball_hit`, bright and quiet (impact t < ~0.25 -> ≤ -8 dB) | `ball_hit` + `ball_body` layer above t 0.55 (unchanged) | `ball_power` + `ball_body` **on the Stingers bus** with a short SFX-bed duck (§3.3) |
| **Kinesthetic** | none | none | existing `Juice` shake 0.3 (FEEL §5.2 Light) + striker rumble 0.6 / 0.5, 0.12 s. Hit-stop deferred to WK-90 |

The audio map needs no change for SOFT / DRIVE. `AudioManager` keys pitch and level off `impact = |Δv|` over 800-3800. A lower floor
and the knee give lower impacts, so soft touches come out brighter and quieter on their own. The range opens without touching the
curve.

### 3.1 Streak trail (the speed indicator)
- **Shows if and only if** the power window is active **and** ball speed > `wk109_trail_min_speed` (2450, normal cap + 50). One meaning:
  "this ball is going faster than any normal touch can make it." It switches off on its own after a damping wall bounce or a blunting
  touch, and fades over 0.1 s.
- **Look (VISION VFX: "thick white cartoon streaks"):** a tapered ribbon behind the ball. Length = speed x 0.06 s (≈ 170 wpx at 2800,
  about 1.4 ball diameters). Width tapers from 0.9 x the ball diameter to 0. White core, **striker's team colour** at the edge
  (DECISIONS WK-24: streak = attacker's colour).
- **Layering:** under the ball (z 40) and under goat sprites, so goats always read on top (Pillar 5). Over the court art. Never
  additive-white across a goat.
- **Must read at couch scale:** art-director grades a 25 %-scaled 1080p frame.

### 3.2 "POWER SHOT!" pop
- A world-space word at the strike point, offset 90 wpx up (toward the screen top when the strike is near the ceiling, so it is never
  clipped).
- Striker team-colour fill, chocolate outline, the UI kit font. Cap height 40 wpx (between WK-131's NOT YET! at 34 and WK-155's OPEN!
  at 46).
- Life 0.6 s: rise 0.1, hold 0.3, fade 0.2.
- **Anti-clutter:** at most one on screen. Match-wide cooldown `wk109_pop_cooldown_s` (1.5 s). Never during STAGE_END or under a goal
  banner. A power shot that scores lets the GOAL banner own the moment, and the pop can be cut short.

### 3.3 Power cue routing (sound-designer)
- `ball_power` (+ the `ball_body` layer) moves to the **Stingers bus**, the WK-163 KO-sting pattern, past the SFX compressor that ate
  3-7 dB off the KO pop. Add a short SFX-bed duck under it: about -3 dB for 0.15 s (KO uses `_start_ko_duck`, which is longer).
- Target: perceived +3 dB or more above the loudest DRIVE touch in the WK-93 `audio.wav`. Power shots are rare (~1-3 per minute), so
  there is no fatigue risk.
- Behind the same master switch: OFF = today's SFX-bus routing.

## 4. Edge cases (programmer's checklist)

1. **Two goats on one tick, one POWER:** power wins the tick. Process POWER touches last (or re-apply the power exit after the tick's
   normal touches). A same-tick normal touch never blunts a power shot that is being struck that tick.
2. **Super-active AND in hitstun** (smashed mid-super): DRIVE. The victim rule wins.
3. **Super striker below 900 px/s:** DRIVE (§2.3).
4. **Power shot into a closed kickoff gate:** it bounces at gate restitution 0.6, and the power window keeps running. 2800 px/s keeps the
   2-tick margin (46.7 < 48), and CCD stays on. Bars: 0 ball-in-gated-mouth ticks, 0 goals before the open (§7.3 G5).
5. **Power shot into the goal:** the latch and the net catch are unchanged. WK-66a net ripple gets a higher `spd`, and the art must
   clamp its reaction (presentation only).
6. **Hazard punt in the window:** reset the cap to normal **before** applying the hazard impulse, so a hammer can never fling a ball to
   2800.
7. **Teammate redirect of a power shot:** blunts it (any non-power touch). Intended: the cannon belongs to the shooter.
8. **Kickoff hang:** a power touch ends the hang like any touch (unchanged path).
9. **Ghosted ball (pre-GO):** no touches, so no power (unchanged).
10. **Trials puzzle balls and Dropshot:** byte-identical. `Ball` defaults the WK-109 path off, and only `SportsMode` turns it on for the
    soccer ball at spawn. Dropshot (`configure_dropshot`) never does.
11. **Own-goal credit threshold:** `sports_own_goal_push` (700 px/s goalward that a defender's touch must ADD) is **not retuned** here.
    The softer floor means fewer grazes clear 700, so the **credited** own-goal count can fall without any change in play. The A/B
    therefore measures own goals two ways (§7.3 G2).
12. **Bots that assume ball ≤ 2400** (intercept / prediction horizons using `BALL_MAX_SPEED` or `max_speed`): the programmer greps
    and reads `ball.max_speed` live. Motion health is the guardrail.
13. **WK-135 S1 touch refuel** (if ruled ON before this runs): pays per accepted touch whatever the tier. No interaction. It must be
    the same value in every arm.

## 5. Tuning knobs (append-only `# WK-109` block in `Tuning.gd`; OFF = today, byte-identical)

CLAUDE.md requires feel constants in `Tuning.gd`, and the harness overrides only `Tuning` vars (`tune_<var>=`). The legacy `Ball.gd`
consts stay as the OFF values.

| Knob | Default | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| `wk109_power_shot` | **false** | MASTER. false = exactly today (impulse 500, no knee, cap 2400, `is_power` = super OR hitstun, SFX bus) |
| `wk109_touch_impulse` | 350.0 | touch floor impulse when ON (legacy const 500) |
| `wk109_touch_knee` | 1600.0 | soft-knee start for non-power touches (px/s). 0 = no knee (arm A1) |

| `wk109_power_hold_s` | 0.8 | s at the power cap after a POWER touch (a full-court 2320 px shot takes 0.83 s) |
| `wk109_power_decay_s` | 0.3 | s the cap eases 2800 -> 2400 after the hold |
| `wk109_trail_min_speed` | 2450.0 | streak shows only above this (normal cap + 50) |
| `wk109_pop_cooldown_s` | 1.5 | match-wide minimum gap between "POWER SHOT!" pops |

Unchanged and PROTECTED: `BALL_DRIVE_GAIN` 0.9, `BALL_SMASH_MULT` 2.0, `BALL_TOUCH_CD` 0.12, `BALL_MAX_SPEED` 2400 (normal cap and
hazards), `BALL_MASS`, restitution, CCD, everything in WK-131 (`sports_kickoff_rule` 1, `sports_gate_open_s` 3.5, gate geometry),
`wk108b_strike_guard` true (K3), `sports_pressure_rule` 0 (WK-116 OFF), the WK-135 phase-3 switches at whatever the director ruled
(identical across arms).

**QA telemetry (presentation-only reads, no sim effect):** per accepted touch `{ball_t, idx, team, cpu, super, stun, tier, p_speed,
v_in, v_out, pos, n_own}`. `n_own` = contact normal · (ball -> own goal). Plus `Ball.qa_trail_on` per tick. `Ball.touched` fires after
the velocity write, so `_ball.linear_velocity` in the handler is `v_out`.

## 9. Fantasy-fit check (pre-build)
- **Is the super the decisive move?** Today in sports it is not (1.4x a normal touch). After this, it is the only way past the normal
  cap: a constant 2800 cannon with a streak and a BOOM. "Only a super stops a super" makes it decisive on defence too.
- **Constant motion?** Yes. Softer touches cushion the ball onto the bouncy floor, and the ball-parked bar stays 0. No new resting
  state.
- **Low floor, high ceiling?** A newcomer still moves the ball on every touch (583 px/s floor). The ceiling gains first-touch control
  at the bottom and a committed cannon at the top.
- **Readable chaos?** Three tiers, three sounds, and one visual (the streak) with exactly one meaning. A victim's pinball touch never
  fakes it.
- **Risk to watch (the human question bots can't answer):** do normal headers now feel limp? R2's 1780 sits close to WK-95's
  "a bit weaker but much more aimable" ~2050 band. The CD replay decides, and the knob order is fixed in §7.5.
