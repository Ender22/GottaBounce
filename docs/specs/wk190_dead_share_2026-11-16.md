## 0. TL;DR

**WK-190 ruling: ACCEPT. No lever. Close with a DECISIONS entry and a restated gate.**
- **CD6-03's premise is wrong by its own data.** I split every human dead second in CD6's 38 stages by phase (§1). The "3-alive middle" holds
  **21 %** of them. The biggest bucket (**49 %**) is the ordinary 2-alive finish *before* any showdown could fire, i.e. watching TWO LEFT! play
  out for a few seconds. **10.5 %** is the 0.5 s double-KO confirmation window after the final KO, a bookkeeping artefact.
- **Nothing in the middle is stalled.** The 3-alive phase has median **1.4 s**, p90 **6.7 s**, max **7.8 s** (n = 37). There's no tail for a
  stall breaker to cut.
- **The share is the format's arithmetic.** For 4 players, 1 life, last-standing, with the 3 eliminations evenly spaced, every seat's expected
  dead share is **(2/3 + 1/3 + 0) / 4 = 25.0 %**. CD6's pooled human dead share, without the double-KO window, is **24.7 %**. Duck Game and
  TowerFall run the same format, so they have the same arithmetic. Their answer is short rounds and, in TowerFall's case, ghosts as an *opt-in*
  variant. Ours: median wait **5.2 s**, p90 **12.7 s**, with ghosts ON by default.
- **Share is scale-invariant.** Any lever that speeds the whole stage up leaves it unchanged. Only three things move it: fewer players, more
  lives (OQ-1, owner only), or reshaping *when* people die (a safe opening or an ended climax, both anti-pillar). The WK-169 director already
  ruled this (DQ-1: "median share is structural under LIVES 1"). WK-190 re-opened a metric that was already ruled on.
- **CD5 25 % → CD6 29 % is not a regression.** WK-169b-2's own pre-showdown lane-N A0 measured a median of **29.4 %** on 18 seat-matches.
  CD5's 6 seat-matches were the low draw.
- **Restless ghosts (L2) stay OFF.** By construction they can't move dead share. They're a felt lever, and nobody has reported a felt failure.
  Lane G is released.
- **Falsifier (190a-v, offline, no Godot):** the same phase split on WK-20d Phase A's 419 raw stages. If the 3-alive phase holds > 30 % of
  human dead seconds or its p90 is > 8 s, 190 reopens as a lever item (§3.4).

**WK-191 ruling: SUDDEN DEATH inherits the SHOWDOWN, keyed to the SD's own clock. Built OFF, decided by a cheap A/B.**
- **The hole:** SD revives the pair pre-damaged Lv2 ("the damage system IS the clock"). But Lv2 heals after `dmg_heal_time` 8.0 s, counted from
  the end of the 0.8 s revive lock (the heal doesn't tick while `frozen`). After that, the SD is a fresh 1v1 with **no clock at all**, two
  ghosts watching, and those ghosts **hushed by K-6**. It's the worst spectator state in the game and the only 1v1 that never escalates.

- **The rule:** new switch `sd_showdown`. **SHOWDOWN enters `sd_showdown_delay` = 7.0 s after the SD unlock**, so the first golden bubble goes

- **Predicted effect:** P(SD duel > 15 s | it reached 7 s) **25-45 % → 6-18 %**. The SD median doesn't move (the lever only acts after 7 s).
  Dead share falls by **≤ 1 pp**. **This is a felt fix for the worst wait, not a share lever,** and I'm saying so up front.
- **A/B (190b):** one headless batch, 2 processes, reusing the `wk169c_recheck` harness plus one scratch arg (`forcesd`) that turns every stage
  into an SD. Arms S0 / S0r / S1, **12 + 8 + 12 seeds × 15 stages = 480 forced SDs, about 20 min wall.** Pre-registered decision table (§6.7).
  It includes a "rare, so accept" row.

**Owner rulings:** none is bent, so no conflict needs logging. LIVES 1, the camera rules, super = the attack, responsive normal flight, "SD only
on a double KO", "no timer ends a stage" and the 8 s heal all stand. OQ-1 and OQ-3 stay parked with the studio recommendation unchanged (no).

## 1. Diagnosis: where the dead seconds actually are (CD6, 38 stages, 9 human seat-matches)

### 1.1 Method
- Source: `round_stats[].spectator.seats[]` (`t_elim`, `dead_s`) and `length_s` from `CD6_data/m1.json`, `m2.json`, `m4.json`. Game time.
- A seat's dead seconds in a stage = `length_s − t_elim`. That's what CD6 reported, and it reproduces its seat shares exactly (m1: 14.4 / 21.5 /
  36.3 %).
- Per stage, the elimination times e1 < e2 < e3 cut the stage into phases. Each human's dead seconds are assigned to the phase they fall in.
- **The double-KO window:** `length_s` runs **0.50 s past the final KO** (`double_ko_window`). The game has to wait that long to know whether
  it's a double KO. Every seat already eliminated "dies" another 0.5 s per stage. Nobody is waiting to play in that window: the KO has
  happened and the stage is decided.
- The two SD stages (m2 Long Meadow, Stepping Bloom) are split at the revive (start of SD estimated from CD6's 12.6 s / 6.2 s SD lengths,
  ±0.3 s). m2's total comes out 1.8 s under round_stats because of that estimate.

### 1.2 Human dead seconds by phase

| Bucket | m1 | m2 | m4 | **Total** | **Share** |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **3 alive** (one ghost; the "middle") | 25.0 | 22.6 | 32.3 | **79.8 s** | **20.8 %** |
| **2 alive, first 6 s** (every tail < 6 s, plus the first 6 s of longer ones; showdown can't act) | 57.0 | 58.5 | 71.2 | **186.7 s** | **48.7 %** |
| **2 alive, inside SHOWDOWN** (7 stages) | 17.7 | 7.5 | 13.7 | **38.8 s** | **10.1 %** |
| **Sudden-death duels** (2 stages, both with 2 human ghosts) | 0 | 37.6 | 0 | **37.6 s** | **9.8 %** |
| **Double-KO window** after the final KO (0.5 s × each dead seat) | 13.4 | 13.0 | 13.8 | **40.2 s** | **10.5 %** |
| **Total** | 113.1 | 139.0 | 131.0 | **383.1 s** | |

Combat time is 462.8 s in total, so human seat-seconds are 1,388.5 s.
- **Pooled human dead share: 27.6 %**, and **24.7 % without the double-KO window.** The seat-match median of 29 % is CD6's number.
- **Long waits are made of single stages, not the middle.** The worst seat (m2 P1, 38 %, 55.9 s) got 15.7 s from Stepping Bloom alone (an 8.5 s
  2-alive tail, then a 6.2 s SD). m1 P3 (36 %) got 25.6 s from one 18.2 s showdown tail.

### 1.3 The middle isn't stalled
3-alive phase durations (e2 − e1), n = 37:
- 0.04, 0.07, 0.17, 0.23, 0.27, 0.32, 0.37, 0.47, 0.51, 0.53, 0.65, 0.66, 0.72, 1.13, 1.17, 1.19, 1.22, 1.41, 1.43, 1.49, 1.61, 1.65, 1.95,
  2.16, 2.31, 2.88, 3.98, 4.06, 4.30, 5.32, 5.44, 5.49, 5.49, 6.63, 6.86, 7.07, 7.82 s;
- **median 1.43 s, p90 ≈ 6.7 s, max 7.82 s.** Only 4 of 37 run past 6 s.

A mid-stage stall breaker with any sane delay (≥ 5 s) would fire on about 5 stages in 38, for 1-2 s each, before the next death that was
coming anyway. **There's no stall to break.** The 3-alive phase is a normal three-way fight, and a three-way fight is the game.

### 1.4 Why the share is structural (the arithmetic)
With N fighters, LIVES 1, last-standing, and eliminations at fractions f_k of the stage length L, each seat's expected dead share is
**Σ (1 − f_k) / N**.

| Elimination shape (N = 4) | f1, f2, f3 | Dead share per seat |
|---|---|---|
| Evenly spaced | 1/3, 2/3, 1 | **25.0 %** |
| CD6 medians (first death 4.3 s, stage 10.9 s, final KO at L − 0.5) | ≈ 0.39, ≈ 0.55, 0.95 | **≈ 27.8 %** |
| CD6 measured, pooled, without the double-KO window | | **24.7 %** |
| An early "first blood" shape (one goat dies fast, the rest late) | 0.15, 0.8, 1 | 26 % |
| A long safe opening (everyone dies in the last third) | 0.7, 0.85, 1 | 11 % |

**Consequences:**
1. **The share is scale-invariant.** Make every stage 30 % faster (more lethal hits, a shrinking arena, faster escalation) and every f_k stays
   put: the share doesn't move, only the absolute waits shrink. That's why WK-169's showdown cut waits (> 15 s waits −42 %) but not the share
   (spec §1.6, P7).
2. **Shortening only the 3-alive phase barely helps.** If the second death comes Δ sooner and everything after shifts with it, the first ghost
   saves Δ and nobody else saves anything. If only the second death moves earlier, total dead time *rises* by Δ.
3. **The only things that lower it** are N (fewer players), LIVES (OQ-1, owner only), or a back-loaded death schedule. That means either a safe
   opening, which is Pillar 2's enemy, or ending the stage before the last 1v1 (first-elimination ends), which kills the climax and the
   owner's last-standing structure.
4. **The worst seat is a skill and luck tail, not a system fault.** Nine equal-skill HumanSim seats already spread from 14 to 38 %. A real couch
   with a skill gap will put its weakest player above 40 %. Every last-standing party game does this. What we control is **how long the wait
   is** and **what the ghost can do**, not who dies first.

### 1.5 Genre comparison (why "accept" is the honest answer)
- **Duck Game:** 1 life, last duck standing, one-hit deaths, rounds about 8-10 s (GAME_DESIGN §16). It's the same format with the same
  ~25 % arithmetic. Dead ducks sit out until the next round. Its comfort lever is **round brevity**, not a dead-player verb.
- **TowerFall:** last archer standing, 1 life by default. Dead archers watch. "Return as Ghosts" is an **opt-in Versus variant**, and its
  ghosts can kill (a bump-kill), which is griefing by our K-1 / K-2 fences.
- **Smash (stock, 3-4 players):** eliminated players spectate for the rest of the game, often minutes.
- **GottaBounce, CD6:** waits median **5.2 s**, p90 **12.7 s**, > 15 s only **2 of 62 (3.2 %)**, max 25.6 s. Ghosts ON by default (float, puff,
  bleat), first puff armed 1.0 s after the ghost appears. **The waits are Duck-Game scale and the dead player has more to do than in either
  reference's default.**
- Pillar 4 ("death is cheap: sub-2 s to next action") is met by the ghost. The KO → ghost-control gap is about 0.6 s (`ghost_spawn_delay`)
  plus the rise.

## 4. WK-191: player fantasy and the hole

> *"Double KO! Pogo and Butch pop back in the middle, both blinking red. SUDDEN DEATH! Me and Ziggy are ghosts and we can't even puff. Hush,
> just watch. They circle... the red pulses on their cards drain... and right as the red runs out the meadow goes quiet, ka-CHUNK, SHOWDOWN!,
> and a golden tank shimmers into the gap. Butch dives, grabs it, three supers later it's over."*

- **The SD should be the shortest, sharpest 1v1 in the game.** It was designed that way: Lv2 pre-damage, central spawns, full fuel, and "the
  damage system IS the clock" (`PartyMatch._sudden_death_revive`).
- **The hole:**
  - `seed_damage(2)` arms an 8.0 s heal timer. The heal doesn't tick while `frozen` (the 0.8 s revive lock), so the clock runs from the
    **unlock**.
  - An SD pair that trades no clean hit for 8 s heals to Lv0 and becomes a **fresh 1v1 with no clock**.
  - Unlike a COMBAT 2-alive tail, it can never escalate: the showdown clears on SD entry (spec 169 §5.3) and doesn't come back.
  - Both ghosts are hushed for the whole SD (K-6).
  - **The rarest duel is also the least-pressured, most-watched one.**
- **Size of the problem:**
  - SD is 7.4-8.1 % of natural Brawl stages (169b-2 lane N, Phase A), about one per first-to-10 match.
  - In CD6, 2 SD stages (5 %) held 9.8 % of human dead seconds, because both had two human ghosts.
  - Long Meadow's ran 12.6 s from the revive (≈ 11.8 s from the unlock), so about 4 s of it was past the heal with no clock.
- **Core verbs exercised:** FLY (the gold race across the court), SUPER-SMASH (the golden tank is super frequency), RECOVER (the non-holder's
  recovery gamble is untouched). **Measured by:** SD duel length, golden collection, the super share of SD-ending KOs, and boost share in the SD
  (§6.5).

## 5. WK-191 mechanic: SD inherits the SHOWDOWN

### 5.1 Rule
With **`sd_showdown` = true** (new; default **false** until the A/B rules), SHOWDOWN starts in `SUDDEN_DEATH` when **all** of these hold:
1. BRAWL, state `SUDDEN_DEATH`, the revive lock has ended (`_sd_lock` ≤ 0; the "SD unlock" moment);
2. the stage started with ≥ 3 fighters (`_stage_start_alive >= 3`): at least one ghost is watching (the same condition as the COMBAT entry);
3. both revived fighters are alive;
4. **`sd_showdown_delay` = 7.0 s** of SD fight time have passed since the unlock (game time, pause-aware);
5. `showdown_enabled` is on.

From entry on, it is **the shipped showdown, unchanged**:
- step 1 at entry, then every `showdown_step_s`, up to `showdown_max_steps`, then it holds;
- a golden drop at entry (1.0 s telegraph), then every `showdown_drop_interval`, at the fair point between the two SD fighters;
- the same tank, tank lock, claim rule, bot seek and GOLD SURGE value as HEAD (whatever WK-188 has ruled by build time);
- the same presentation: SHOWDOWN! band, ka-CHUNK, music hush with the LPF steps, dim, rims, gold card frames, holder glint;
- the step counter and LPF restart at step 1 even if this stage already had a COMBAT showdown before the double KO (Stepping Bloom's case).

**Exit:** the stage ends (one fighter left, or a second double KO with no survivor; one revive per stage, unchanged) → the eased release, as
for any stage end.

### 5.2 Why 7.0 s (the number)
- **`sd_showdown_delay` = `dmg_heal_time` (8.0) − `showdown_drop_telegraph` (1.0) = 7.0 s, counted from the unlock.**
  - The first golden bubble goes live exactly when an un-hit pair's Lv2 would heal.
  - The HUD already shows that clock: the WK-31 DANGER! sticker's heal bar drains on both cards. So the gold arrives as the bar empties.
    **One visible clock hands over to the next.** No new UI, nothing to count.
- **Why not 6.0 (= `showdown_delay`, "one number to learn")?** The gold would land 1 s before the heal, while both are still Lv2. A golden super
  on a Lv2 goat is near-certain death, which recreates CD6-01's point-blank coin flip on top of the SD's own clock (double pressure).
- **Why not 0 (gold at once)?**
  - The pair spawns at the two most central spawns, near each other and usually near the fair point. That's the CD6-01 "decided by where you
    floated" race at its worst.
  - It would also replace the SD's damage clock instead of extending it.
- **Why not "never heal in SD"?** A heal pause bends the owner's damage directive §3 ("heals after 8 s with no new hit"). That's OQ-3
  territory, parked with the recommendation no. The showdown gets the same pressure without touching damage.
- **Why not hold off until the heal actually happens?** A hit inside the SD re-arms the victim's 8 s timer. Keying the entry to the per-goat
  heal would make the trigger depend on combat details nobody can read. A fixed 7.0 s from the unlock is simple and readable, and it matches the
  common case (no clean hit yet).

### 5.3 Edge cases
- **Stepping Bloom's case (COMBAT showdown → double KO → SD):** the showdown clears at SD entry as today (instant hush release, golden bubbles
  pop). It re-enters at unlock + 7.0 s as a fresh step 1. "GOLDEN TANK!" (the first-drop band) shows **on the first drop of each entry**, so at
  most twice per stage.
- **No double banner:** "SUDDEN DEATH!" holds 0.8 s at the revive. "SHOWDOWN!" plays ≥ 7.0 s later. They can never share a frame. The A/B
  telemetry and the acceptance test assert it (T-191c).
- **K-6 SD hush stays.** Ghosts stay hushed for the whole SD (no puffs). They get the showdown's banner, sound and 0.1 / 0 rumble, which is
  exactly the "something's happening" beat a hushed ghost lacks today. L2 is OFF; if it ever ships, K-6 still overrides it in SD.
- **Regular powerups stay stopped in SD** (`PowerupSpawnDirector.on_sudden_death`, unchanged). The golden drop is the showdown's own object and
  the only item that can appear in an SD.
  - The SD is still "decided by the revived pair alone": the gold is a fighter-side prize, and nothing ends by fiat.
  - The claim rule (169c §2.8 b) has nothing to claim: no regular bubbles exist.
- **2-fighter stages** (a 2-player match, or a stage that started with 2): no entry. Nobody is watching, which is the same rule as COMBAT.
- **Match point:** the SD showdown runs (fighter-side, fair). Ghosts are hushed anyway.
- **LIVES > 1:** an SD only happens on last lives, so the rule is identical.
- **A pre-damage hit inside the SD** re-arms that goat's heal. It doesn't move the entry (§5.2).
- **The holder dies** → the stage ends (1 alive), or a second double KO ends it with no survivor.
- **Pause / disconnect overlay:** every SD and showdown clock freezes.
- **Sports / Trials:** untouched (Brawl only, as today).
- **Camera:** untouched. The showdown adds no focus point (169c T-5a grep).

## 6. 190b: the WK-191 A/B (one headless batch, ≤ 2 Godot processes)

### 6.1 Why a forced-SD lane
- Natural SDs are 7-8 % of stages, and the lever only touches those that reach 7 s.
- A plain lane-T rig makes SDs 11-15 % of stages (169b-2). That would need about 1,200 stages per arm to see 50 SDs past 7 s.
- **So the harness forces the SD itself.** It's still a faithful replica:
  - the revive path is the real one: the two central spawns, full fuel, Lv2, the 0.8 s lock, the unlock;
  - the only thing forced is *how* the double KO happened, and nothing about the double KO carries into the SD;
  - the ghosts are hushed (K-6) in both the natural and the forced case.

### 6.2 Prerequisites
1. **191-prep** (gameplay-programmer, lane D, code only, outside the cap): the §5 rule behind `sd_showdown` (default false), the §5.4 constants,
   and the §5.5 telemetry. Parse gate.
   - **OFF parity:** with `sd_showdown=false`, a 4-bot 5-stage smoke run with one forced SD has the same state sequence and phase_log as HEAD.
   - Commit, plus a STATUS line.
2. **Harness** (QA, scratch only): copy `tools/out/wk/wk169c_recheck/` → `tools/out/wk/wk190b_batch/`. Add one recorder arg to
   `WK169bRec.gd.txt`:

     `KillPipeline.kill(p, &"wk190_forced_sd", null)` on seats 0 and 1 **in the same physics frame**;
   - flag the stage `t2_class = "forced_sd"`;
   - if the SD revive doesn't follow within 1 frame of the double-KO decision, flag `sd_fail` (must be 0);
   - stages where seat 0 or 1 died before `forcesd` are `natural` and excluded.

## 9. Fantasy-fit check (GAME_ESSENCE)
- **Airborne and using the jetpack?** 190: unchanged (CD6: living humans 100 % airborne, boosting 26-36 %). 191: the gold is a flight race, and
  BS guards it.
- **Do distances require boost or super?** The fair point sits between the SD pair. By 7 s they've usually drifted apart, so it's a real flight.
- **Is the super the decisive kill?** The SD's late phase becomes golden-super territory (SS info line). The COMBAT super share is unaffected.
- **Constant motion, no camping?** A pair circling after the heal now gets a reason to converge, and the CD6 12.6 s duel is exactly that case.
- **Pillar 4 (death is cheap)?** 190: ghost control within about 0.6 s of the KO, waits median 5.2 s. 191: caps the one wait where the ghost
  can't even puff.
- **Pillar 5 (readable)?** No new UI. The existing DANGER! heal bar *is* the countdown to the gold, read off the card the player already
  watches. Gold, never red. No clock digits. No second banner on top of SUDDEN DEATH!.
- **First-time human?** "SUDDEN DEATH!" means one hit settles it. If it doesn't, "SHOWDOWN!" and a gold thing appear: go get it. It's the same
  sentence the game already teaches in every long 1v1.

## 10. Pushback recorded
- **CD6-03's framing ("most dead time is the 3-alive middle"): refuted by §1.2.** It's 21 %, and the middle has no stall tail (§1.3).
- **WK-190 re-opens DQ-1.** The director ruled the median share structural under LIVES 1 and restated the WK-169 accept around seconds and long
  waits. This spec reaches the same answer with CD6's data and a closed-form reason (§1.4). The gate in §3.2 makes it stick: share is
  *reported*, waits are *gated*.
- **"Restless ghosts fix the dead share":** they can't, by construction (§2). Shipping a lever against a metric it can't move would be
  measuring the wrong thing.
- **Any shrinking, squeezing or closing pressure to "speed up the middle":** it's the laser wall, and it doesn't move a scale-invariant share
  anyway.
- **Gold at SD entry (delay 0):** a point-blank coin flip between two Lv2 goats at the central spawns. That's CD6-01 at its worst.
- **Pause the heal in SD:** it bends the owner's damage directive. The showdown handover gets the pressure without it.
