## 0. At a glance

**Ruling: yes, the tail is a real (moderate) couch problem, concentrated on the big court. Fix it with ONE rule: PRESSURE NETS.**
- After **30 s** of live ball with no goal, both goal mouths **widen 25 %** with a loud, readable beat ("PRESSURE!").
- At **40 s** they widen to **+50 %** ("MAX PRESSURE!") and the crowd starts a clap-chant until somebody scores.
- The next kickoff starts with normal mouths. That happens inside the existing stage-per-point reload, so the 2.667 s restart and the
  kickoff gates are untouched by construction.
- Dropshot, Brawl and Trials: untouched.

**Why this rule:** it acts only on the tail (~7-10 % of rallies reach 30 s). It leaves the median alone. It gives a long rally a climax
instead of cutting it off. And it rewards the SUPER-SMASH verb, because a bigger target pays off the committed power shot.

**Rejected:** hot ball / speed-up, shrinking goals, a point multiplier, a springier floor, LONG HAUL-only bumpers, and "do nothing,
Rocket League style". Reasons are in §3.

## 1. The fantasy and the core verbs

**Player fantasy:** "We've been going end to end for half a minute, the crowd is losing it, the goals just got BIGGER. Whoever lands
the next super-shot wins the point." A long rally should feel like a **climax that is building**, not a grind that flatlines.

| Verb | How PRESSURE NETS exercises it | How we measure it being used |
|---|---|---|
| FLY / BOOST | Unchanged. Mouths stay above a floor-bouncer's apex at max size (§4.3), so carrying the ball in still needs flight | human boost share within ±5 pt of the OFF arm |
| SUPER-SMASH | A wider mouth turns more committed power shots into goals | share of post-30 s goals whose last touch was a super (info, expect ≥ OFF arm) |
| BOUNCE | Untouched (floor, walls, ball restitution unchanged) | ball-parked episodes = 0, touches per rally ≥ 0.9 × OFF |
| RECOVER | n/a in sports | n/a |

## 2. Is the long tail a problem? (evidence + couch reasoning)

### 2.1 Numbers (WK-131 A/B batch, HumanSim3 × 3 + CPU L2, 2v2, game time; I extracted every `ball_t`)

| Sample | Rallies | Median | ≥ 30 s | ≥ 40 s | ≥ 45 s | Time spent beyond 30 s, as a share of all rally time |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| CENTER PITCH, gates 3.5 (A1b, the shipped rule) | 72 | 10.4 s | 5 (6.9 %): 32.5, 33.9, 36.7, 40.9, 63.9 | 2 | 1 | 58 s of 992 s = **5.8 %** |
| LONG HAUL, gates 3.0 (LH1) | 30 | 9.6 s | 3 (10 %): 31.0, 34.3, 83.8 | 1 | 1 | 59 s of 452 s = **13 %** |
| LONG HAUL, legacy (LH0) | 21 | 10.6 s | 1 (32.6) | 0 | 0 | — |
| Pooled CENTER (A1b) + LH1 | 102 | ~10 s | 8 (7.8 %) | 3 | 2 (2.0 %) | residual time beyond 30 s: mean 14.6 s, median 5.5 s |

Earlier samples, from before the WK-107 LONG HAUL re-cut: 61.3 s and 81.8 s out of 11 (M3), plus 31 s and 43 s on CENTER (M1).

### 2.2 What it means for a couch
- **The median is healthy.** 10 s rallies are the game working. Nothing in this spec may move it.
- **Rocketball has no match clock.** The match is first-to-N, so nothing caps the tail. In Rocket League, the 5-minute clock bounds
  the session no matter how long one rally runs. We don't have that, so "Rocket League style, do nothing" is not an equivalent choice.
- **How often a group hits the tail.** With ~9-16 rallies per match, the chance that a match contains a 45 s+ rally is about 12 % on
  CENTER and about 25 % on LONG HAUL (from the ≥ 45 s rates above). So a quarter of LONG HAUL matches include a rally that runs
  three to eight times the median. An 84 s rally is a third of a typical match.
- **Long rallies are real play.** They had 57-113 touches and the ball never parked (WK-131 audit). So the problem is not "stuck". The
  problem is that **nothing escalates**. Minute two of a rally feels exactly like second ten: same nets, same sound, no stakes.
  Party games solve this with an escalation beat, as in Smash's sudden-death bombs, Mario Kart's final lap or Lethal League's speed
  ramp. That beat turns "will this ever end" into "OH NO, the nets!".
- **Verdict:** moderate on LONG HAUL, mild on CENTER, not a blocker anywhere. P2 is the right priority. The fix must be a **tail-only
  escalation**, not a general change to how scoring works.

## 4. The rule: PRESSURE NETS

### 4.1 Mechanic
- **Rally clock.** The rally clock is `ball_t`: the same physics/ball clock as `GoalZone.scored_ball_t`. It starts at GO, pauses during
  hit-stop, pause and the disconnect overlay, and is game time only.
- **Steps**, applied to both mouths at once (symmetric pressure: neither team is favoured):

  | Time on `ball_t` | Mouth height | What happens |
  |---|---|---|
  | < `t1 − warn` (27 s) | base | nothing; the rule is inert |
  | `t1 − warn` → `t1` (27-30 s) | base | anticipation cue only (§5) |
  | **≥ `t1` (30 s)** | **base × 1.25** | **STEP 1 "PRESSURE!"** |
  | **≥ `t2` (40 s)** | **base × 1.50** | **STEP 2 "MAX PRESSURE!"**, then the chant until the goal |

- **How a mouth widens.**
  - The two post columns (upper and lower) **retract** away from the mouth centre, symmetrically. They tween over `slide_s` (0.30 s,
    ease-out), and their colliders move with the visual.
  - Retraction only ever turns solid post into open air, so it can't trap or squash a goat or the ball.
  - The **back post** extends so it still spans the mouth ±100 px. That keeps the WK-122 pocket sealed: the 60 px slot stays narrower
    than any goat or the ball.
  - The **goal_zone** rect grows to match the new mouth height. Its x-extent is unchanged.
- **Reset.** The next kickoff's stage load builds base-size mouths: stage-per-point already rebuilds the court. No tween and no extra
  time. If the build does **not** reload per point, the snap back to base must happen inside the existing `sports_goal_freeze`
  (1.2 s), while the screen is frozen and covered by the goal banner. **No phase timing may change.**

### 4.2 Per-court numbers (base → step 1 → step 2)

| Court | Mouth (base) | Step 1 (+25 %) | Step 2 (+50 %) | Lower-edge check (§4.3) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| CENTER PITCH (and the 5 courts that share its pocket) | 300 px, y650..950 | 375, y612..988 | **450, y575..1025** | apex centre y ≈ 1036 (floor 1350 − r 44 − apex 270) → bottom 1025 is above it ✓ (11 px) |
| LONG HAUL | 340 px, y780..1120 | 425, y738..1162 | **510, y695..1205** | apex centre y1246 (court JSON) → bottom 1205 ✓ (41 px) |
| THE BOX | builder computes | | | per-court check required |

- On LONG HAUL, step 2 brings the mouth bottom down to 355 px above the floor. The ball's measured traffic band is 350-630 px above
  the floor (`long_haul.json`, WK-98 M3 log). That is exactly why LONG HAUL grinds: the ball lives just under the mouth. So most of
  the effect lands where the tail is worst, without a court-specific rule.

### 4.3 Constraints on the grown mouth (checked per court, data-driven)
1. **Flight is still required to score.** At max size, the mouth's bottom edge must stay above the centre line of a trampoline-floor
   bouncer's apex. If symmetric growth would cross it, clamp the lower edge there and give the rest of the growth to the upper edge.
2. **The pocket stays sealed.** No goat or ball ever reaches the region outside the mouth band (the WK-122 probe, run at max size).
3. **No collision with other geometry.** A retracting post must not open into a neighbouring bumper or hazard. Optional per-court
   override in the court JSON: `"pressure_max_grow_px"`. It defaults to the Tuning fractions.
4. **Gate fit.** The kickoff gate only ever exists at base size, because gates drop at 3.5 s and pressure starts at 27 s. Nothing
   resizes gates.

## 5. Feedback: what players see, hear and feel (FEEL §5 Rule of Three: visual + audio + kinesthetic)

| Beat | Visual | Audio | Kinesthetic |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Anticipation** (27 → 30 s) | The goal-frame trim on both mouths starts a slow pulse, cream → orange at 1.5 Hz. No text | The crowd bed swells +6 dB over 3 s (rising murmur) | — |
| **STEP 1 "PRESSURE!"** (30 s) | Word burst "PRESSURE!" in the sports word style (WK-117 family): team-neutral gold, top-centre, 0.9 s. Must not cover either mouth. The posts slide apart (0.30 s), the net mesh stretches, and vertical up/down chevrons flash beside each mouth for 1.0 s. The trim stays orange | A two-hit mechanical "ka-CHUNK" per mouth, plus a crowd "OOOH" | Light shake (+0.25 trauma, FEEL §5.2 "Light") if shake is on in Options; rumble 0.2 / 0.35 for 0.08 s ×2 on every pad (FEEL §5.5 bounce thump, doubled = the clunk) |
| **STEP 2 "MAX PRESSURE!"** (40 s) | Word burst "MAX PRESSURE!" (same slot, bigger, red-gold). The posts slide again. The trim turns red with a heat shimmer and a faster pulse (3 Hz) | A heavier "ka-CHUNK", then a rhythmic crowd clap-chant (≈ 2 claps/s) that **loops until the goal** | Shake +0.25 and rumble as at step 1 |
| **Goal under pressure** | The normal goal celebration and the WK-124 scorer banner, unchanged (its readability is PROTECT-listed) | The chant resolves into the normal goal roar | normal |
| **Reset** | Mouths are base size and the trim is cream at the next kickoff (hidden inside the reload) | the chant stops at the goal | — |

**Readability rules:**
- The posts themselves are the readout, so there is no HUD meter. Sports uses whole-court framing, so both mouths are always on

- **Banner stacking.** If a WK-110 MATCH POINT / GOLDEN GOAL banner is showing (future), the PRESSURE burst waits 0.5 s after it or
  takes the slot below it. Two banners never overlap.
- **Colour.** The orange/red trim must contrast with the yellow goal trim and the WK-131 gate pole (CD4-05c). The art-director grades
  it at couch scale (1/4-size frame).
- **RULES row** (ui-ux, only if shipped): Rocketball RULES gets "PRESSURE NETS: ON / OFF", with per-mode memory and default ON. One
  line in the stage-1 rule strip: "LONG RALLY? THE NETS GROW."

## 6. Tuning knobs (`Tuning.gd`, a new WK-116 block in the sports section; lane S owns it)

| Knob | Default for the A/B | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| `sports_pressure_rule: int` | **0** (OFF; the A/B sets 1) | One switch. 0 is byte-identical to today |
| `sports_pressure_t1_s: float` | **30.0** | ≈ 3 × the median rally (10 s) and 1.5 × the top of the WK-116 band (20 s). In the data, 7.8 % of rallies reach it, so it is a tail rule, not a scoring change |
| `sports_pressure_t2_s: float` | **40.0** | 10 s later: long enough for step 1 to have a fair chance (expected ~1 goal attempt per 3-5 s in late rallies), and early enough that a rally reaching 45 s has faced max nets for 5 s |
| `sports_pressure_warn_s: float` | **3.0** | One readable anticipation beat. Long enough for the crowd swell to register, short enough that it doesn't get tuned out |
| `sports_pressure_grow1_frac: float` | **0.25** | A clearly visible move (+75 px on CENTER, about 1.7 goat heights). Smaller reads as a glitch |
| `sports_pressure_grow2_frac: float` | **0.50** | Max mouth = 39 % of CENTER's interior height (450 / 1140). That's a big target without becoming a lottery, and it stays inside §4.3 on both measured courts. **Pre-registered step: 0.75** (§10.3) |
| `sports_pressure_slide_s: float` | **0.30** | Slower than a hit (0.12 s recover, FEEL §5.1) so the eye can follow the posts, and faster than a rally beat |

**Invariants to record next to the knobs:**
- `t1 − warn` > `sports_gate_open_s` (3.5). Pressure and gates can never overlap.
- `t2` > `t1`.
- The grown mouth's lower edge is above the court's floor-bouncer apex centre (§4.3).

## 7. Edge cases

| Case | Behaviour |
|---|---|
| Goal scored during the 0.30 s slide | Counts normally; reset as usual |
| Ball centre already inside the newly grown zone area on the step tick | Impossible by geometry (that area was solid post, and a ball against the post face has its centre ≥ 60 px outside the zone's x-extent). Defence in depth: the latch requires a **fresh entry** after the step tick. Counted as `pressure_instant_goals`, which must be 0 |
| Goat touching a post face when it retracts | It just gains space. Retraction never pushes. `pressure_squash_ticks` must be 0 |
| Own goal under pressure | Counts (CD4-09 watch: measured separately, §9 P7) |
| Pause / disconnect overlay (WK-28b) / hit-stop | `ball_t` freezes, so steps and the chant freeze. The chant loop pauses with the audio bus |
| Ball escape / respawn mid-rally (if it ever happens) | The rally clock continues and pressure stays. The ball respawns at the court's kickoff point and normal mouths do not return |
| 1v1 and 2v2 | Same rule |
| A goal at exactly 29.99 s | No pressure ever happened; nothing to reset |
| Sudden death / overtime states (none today; WK-110 GOLDEN GOAL is future) | Pressure is independent of match state. If WK-110 adds a golden-goal phase, its designer decides whether pressure starts earlier there. Not in this spec |
| Dropshot | **Excluded** (no mouths; rally median 9-11 s, max 25.7 s: healthy). The rule is read only on the soccer path |
| Trials ball goals | Excluded (Trials code path) |
| Brawl | Excluded |
| Bots | No new logic. They aim at the goal-zone centre, which doesn't move. Keepers may be slightly mispositioned under max nets: accepted, symmetric |
| Courts with hazards near a mouth | Per-court check §4.3.3; `pressure_max_grow_px` override |

## 8. What this must not touch
- **Restart:** goal → GO stays exactly 2.667 s (median and max), the PROTECT line. No phase timing changes.
- **Kickoff gates (WK-131):** `sports_kickoff_rule`, `sports_gate_open_s` 3.5, the gate entity, the padlock and the latch are
  unchanged. The rule is inert before 27 s, so every kickoff metric must match the OFF arm within noise.
- **WK-109 / `Ball.gd`:** no ball constant changes. This is why the hot ball was rejected.
- **WK-135c** (sports ball-touch refuel): no fuel interaction. If 135c lands mid-batch, stop and re-baseline.
- Brawl, Trials, Dropshot: byte-identical paths.
- Camera: sports whole-court framing is unchanged (the mouths are inside the court bounds at every size).
