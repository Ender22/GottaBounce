# WK-17 font brief: bundled display + body face (2026-11-14, ui-ux-designer)

## Decision
| Role | Face | File | Licence |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Display** (theme `default_font`; every title, button, banner, HUD number, caps label) | **Lilita One** (Regular, single heavy weight) | `assets/fonts/lilitaone/LilitaOne-Regular.ttf` | SIL OFL 1.1, `assets/fonts/lilitaone/OFL.txt` (Reserved Font Name "Lilita") |
| **Body** (mixed-case sentences and dense small text, dark on cream) | **Fredoka SemiBold** (variable font pinned to wght 600 by a `FontVariation`) | `assets/fonts/fredoka/Fredoka-Variable.ttf` (upstream name `Fredoka[wdth,wght].ttf`, renamed to drop the brackets from the res:// path) | SIL OFL 1.1, `assets/fonts/fredoka/OFL.txt` |

Both files come unmodified from `github.com/google/fonts` (`ofl/lilitaone`, `ofl/fredoka`), checked as real TrueType files
(magic `00010000`, read by fontTools: Lilita One v1.002 with 225 glyphs, Fredoka v2.001 with wght 300-700 and wdth 75-125 axes).
DECISIONS ruling (b) covers bundling them. Lilita has a Reserved Font Name, so we ship the file unchanged and never
subset or rename the family.

## Why these two
- **Lilita One is the party-brawler house face.** It is heavy, has soft rounded corners, and is slightly condensed. It has
  the same sticker energy as Brawl Stars, Fall Guys and Mario Kart and is less cartoony than Luckiest Guy. It looks good
  under a thick chocolate outline (our CC-8.2 "text over art" treatment), and mixed case still reads at 28 px.
- **It is narrower than what we render today.** On the studio machine the old `SystemFont` chain fell through to
  **Segoe UI Black** (Arial Rounded MT Bold is not installed). Measured in-engine across 298 labels, the median width is now
  **0.76x** the old width, and caps look the same size because the cap height is the same (0.70 em). Nothing that fitted
  before can overflow now. The headless layout scan flagged zero unwrapped labels wider than their rect, before or after.
- **Fredoka for body text** has the same rounded toy feel but is lighter, with open counters and a large x-height. The goat
  flavour lines ("Come at me. I've been stretching.") and the rules summary bar are sentences, and heavy Lilita at 28 px
  closes up "e/a/g" much like the outline did (CD1-05). Two faces with fixed jobs counts as one type system, not two styles.
- Rejected: **Baloo 2** (very tall vertical metrics, so every fixed-height pill would shift), **Luckiest Guy** (caps only,
  very wide, overflow risk), **Nunito** (clean but reads "web app", not toybox), **Titan One** (wider than Segoe Black).

## Wiring (one source of truth)
- `assets/ui/theme.tres`: the `SystemFont_gbchunky` sub-resource is gone. `default_font` is the Lilita `FontFile`, and a
  `FontVariation_body` (Fredoka, `wght` = 600) feeds the new body style.
- `project.godot` `[gui] theme/custom_font` points at Lilita. Anything outside the theme tree (dev overlays, any stray
  Control) also gets the bundled face, never a system font (CC-12.3).
- No scene or script had a per-node font override (grep showed no `theme_override_fonts`, `FontFile` or `SystemFont`
  outside the theme), so nothing fights the theme.

## Outline rule (fixes CD1-05, applies everywhere)
Root cause: the base `Label` theme type has a chocolate outline. Every lobby or pause label that set its *font colour* to
chocolate inherited a **chocolate outline on chocolate text**, a fake-bold effect that fills in the counters.
- **New theme types.** `TextDark` (display face, chocolate, outline 0, 28 px) for caps labels on cream. `BodyDark` (Fredoka
  600, chocolate, outline 0, 28 px) for sentences and dense small text on cream. Scene font sizes still override per node.
- **Applied to:** HerdLobby (BACK, CPUs, CPU tags, NONE, RULES and rules summary text), LobbySlot (device name, blurb,
  flair value, LOCK IN, the 4 rules row titles, DONE/UNDO), RosterMedallion P-badges, and the Pause context line.
- **Small white text over art** keeps the chocolate outline but at the CC-8.2 floor of **6 px** (was 8): `Hint`,
  `CardName` and the base `Label`. Big display text (banners, titles, NameTag, pips) keeps its heavy outline. Dark text with
  a *white* outline (ShellBtn, CardTag, ModeTitle) is left alone because a light outline on cream does no harm.
- **Rule for every future screen:** chocolate text on a cream plate uses `TextDark` / `BodyDark` (no outline). White text
  over art uses a chocolate outline of 6 px or more. Never put an outline the same colour as the text.

## Known gaps / follow-ups
- **The check mark (U+2713) is in neither face.** The `MenuCard` check (150 px) still renders through FontFile system
  fallback, just as it did under SystemFont. Follow-up for the programmer: replace that Label with the drawn `PadGlyph`
  "check" glyph so CC-12.3 holds fully. No other non-ASCII character in player-facing strings is missing (`· — ©` are covered).
- Line boxes are tighter (Lilita 1.14 em vs Segoe Black 1.33 em) and caps now sit truly centred, about 0.06 em higher than
  before. Fixed-height pills and chips may look a few px different. Check them in the screenshots, but none overflow.
- Text below the CC-8.1 floor (SportsHUD `CapLabel` 15 px, TrialsHUD `SubLabel` 20 px, ScoreScreen `Sub` 24 px,
  CardTag 22 px, PadGlyph letters 20-24 px) belongs to WK-18, which this item unblocks.
- Fonts import as dynamic (not MSDF), as before. Scale-tweened banners stay as sharp as they are today.
